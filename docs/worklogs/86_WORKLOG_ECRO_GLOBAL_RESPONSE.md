## ECRO v0 as implemented (frozen in `experiments/ecro86/protocol/ecro86.json`)

**Operator contract.**

- T = S_ER C_G, q = S_ER e. The reference integrator truncates at D = 6 scattering events: b = Σ_{j=0}^{5} T^j q.
- LOW = q + T q is computed physically (camera vertex x1 with next-event estimation, one ER-sampled bounce to x2 with next-event estimation).
- The higher-order target is H[q] = Σ_{j=2}^{5} T^j q (camera paths with 3–6 scattering events).
- ECRO computes b̂ = q + T q + T R_θ(G, z_ER)[e]. The last flight T of the higher-order term is the x1 → x2 bounce that LOW traces anyway. R_θ[e](x2, ω) approximates the radiance leaving x2 towards x1 made of 2–5 scattering events, so the camera reads `throughput(x1) × B[patch(x2), bin(ω)]` with no additional ray.
- Event correspondence: a next-event contribution at the k-th camera-path vertex has exactly k scattering events (e1 = q, e2 = T q, h = events 3–6). Mitsuba's own path integrator with `max_depth = k + 1` is the same truncation (test V1 at k = 6, 2, 1).

**Ownership.**

- ER owns every scattering event evaluated physically and conditions the operator only through its frozen compiled states.
- Current geometry owns visibility: camera ray, the x1 → x2 flight, light visibility in e1 / e2 and in the source encoding, and the state rays.
- ECRO owns only paths with ≥ 3 scattering events. The table excludes the once-scattered radiance of x2 (that is e2), so nothing is counted twice.

**Representation (one family, fixed before any evidence).**

- 1,270 surface patches (25 cm grid on floor / ceiling / walls, 20 cm on the panel, 3 × 3 per box face, 8 × 4 on the sphere) × 64 equal-solid-angle bins of the patch-local hemisphere, piecewise constant.
- Source encoding `s` [1270, 64, 3]: patch-averaged direct irradiance by incident-direction bin (16 shadow-ray samples per patch per light).
- Output table `B` [1270, 64, 3]: outgoing radiance of 2–5 scattering events by outgoing-direction bin.
- Geometry state: per patch 512 stratified hemisphere rays (8 per incident bin) storing the hit patch and the outgoing bin at the hit patch. After a rigid edit only rays whose segment crosses the old or new box volume, or that start on the moved box, are re-traced (selective update); a full rebuild is the FULL-STATE control.

**Learned operator (`model86.py`, 0.74 M parameters).**

- Generator inputs: patch tables, frozen ER state of each patch, state rays. It never sees radiance.
- Patch tokens (3 rounds of message passing over the state-ray form-factor matrix) → non-negative factors: `c` (incident bin → 16 channels), `a` (16 channels → outgoing bin), a rank-24 global encoder / decoder.
- Application, linear in `s`: `u = c·s`; one learned flight through the state rays (current visibility) `E1 = Σ_rays (a·u)[hit, bin] cos dΩ`; `B = a·(c·E1 + P·(Σ_j area_j Q_j·u_j))`.
- Zero input → zero, exact superposition and non-negative output hold by construction. No single-scattering passivity is imposed on the multi-flight response.

**Supervision.** Path-traced frozen-ER field (2–5 scattering events) per patch and bin, for random box poses / sizes and single white point lights of one room family. Test geometries and lights are excluded with margins (0.15 m per box, 0.3 m per light). No image, no test configuration, no source identity.

## Workload and gates (prospective)

- **Fixture.** A semi-enclosed 4 × 4 × 3 m room (open towards the camera): ER floor (B), sharp ER panel (S1), moderate ER sphere (S2), Lambert walls / ceiling, two movable Lambert boxes, point light.
- **E1** (primary, inspected during development): box A translates 1.1 m; at T0 it blocks the light → panel beam.
- **E2** (independent, no radiance computed before the frozen chain): the other box translates and rotates in front of the panel, with another light.
- **Held-out sources** on the E1 T1 geometry: LMIX (two coloured lights) and LSPOT (a hard-edged cone, a source class never trained on).
- **Regions** from geometry / visibility only (`masks86.py`): A direct-visibility change; C mirror footprint enabled / blocked or panel / sphere receivers whose mirror cone sees a change; D broad receivers with unchanged direct visibility and exposure ≥ 5 %; E exposure ≤ 1 %.
- **Stage 0** (REF-P): Q1–Q4, Q6, Q7 for E1 and E2 + renderer validation, else `STOPPED_UNQUALIFIED_WORKLOAD`.
- **Stage 1**: H share ≥ 5 % and resolvable; oracle projection of the patch × bin table E_H ≤ 0.02 (stationary) and ≤ 0.04 (A, C, D), capture ≤ 0.25.
- **Stage 3**: learned operator E_H ≤ 0.03 (stationary), ≤ 0.06 (A, C, D), capture ≤ 0.25, unseen sources ≤ 0.04, higher-order change slope 0.75–1.25 and correlation ≥ 0.8 in D, field-level validation ≤ 1.5 × the training subset, operator contracts, STALE and ablation controls, two seeds.
- **Stage 4**: matched-quality edit-to-first-frame and per-frame cost against plain path tracing at 0.10 / 0.05 / 0.03 / g, with region-matched errors.
- **Error measure.** E_H = Σ|hl − h_REF-ER| / Σ tot_REF-ER: the additional error of the higher-order term relative to total radiance. The LOW estimator is shared by ECRO and the reference, so its noise does not enter.

## Pre-freeze development (non-evidence)

1. **Fixture look render** (E1, 64 spp): the phenomenon is in view; ≥ 3-event share about 24 % of the radiance.
2. **Dev oracle projection** (E1 T1, 1,024 spp): the patch × bin table reproduces the higher-order term without mean bias (hl / h 0.99–1.00 per object); total-image error 0.030 against a LOW-noise level of 0.027 at that spp. ECRO's render costs 0.34 ms GPU per spp against 1.81 for full path tracing.
3. **Dev learning** (48-geometry dev split, seed 86300; 40 train / 8 val, 4,000 steps, 128 s): validation field error 0.14 against a target-noise level of 0.105; without the global term 0.39, without the single-flight term 0.64. Architecture, loss and schedule were fixed here.
4. **Dev-model render** (E1 T1): ECRO ≈ oracle projection within noise; STALE was close except in A and on the panel. The STALE criterion was therefore declared as a control ("the update matters somewhere"), not as a requirement in every region.
5. **Corrections found by dev runs.**
   - Two scenes in one Dr.Jit kernel disable OptiX ray tracing silently (masks were all "changed"); every per-scene query is now evaluated separately.
   - An empty dirty set (null edit) must skip the trace.
   - The patch round-trip test counted the box face lying on the floor (coplanar with it); it now compares hits on the same shape only.
   - Test geometries were moved slightly so that they lie inside the training distribution's support (box–sphere clearance rule).
6. **High-sample dev oracle projection** (E1 T1, reference 2 × 8,192 spp, table 4,096 spp): E_H 0.005 on the stationary pixels (H share 0.237; noise of the reference term 0.004), 0.013 in A, 0.010 in C, 0.005 in D, 0.019 on the panel, 0.029 in the small class E. The Stage 1 thresholds (0.02 / 0.04) were already written when this was measured.
7. **End-to-end dev smoke of the chain** (`run86.sh --dev`: 128 spp, 24 training geometries, 600 steps). Every stage and gate ran without error; all validation tests passed (V1–V8); gates failed as expected at that sample count. Two changes followed, both before the freeze:
   - The selective state update spent 0.53 s in a numpy dirty test against 0.03 s for a full rebuild. The dirty test now runs on the GPU (selective 23 ms, full 29 ms; identical state).
   - Q3 measured the sharp / moderate share of the *static* ≥ 2-event radiance in C (0.20 on E1, diluted by broad multi-bounce light). It now measures the share of the *change* of that radiance, which is the quantity the class is meant to capture.
   - **Disclosure.** The smoke also ran E2 at 128 spp, so its noise-dominated aggregate numbers were seen. They were used for one decision only: REF-P replicas 4 → 8, so that the change of the higher-order term in D can be resolved. E2 stays excluded from training; masks, thresholds and the representation were fixed without it.

## Frozen chain, first run (commit `7786d09`, 2026-11-11 01:27–02:12 UTC): stopped at the Stage 0 gate

- **Outcome by the frozen rule: `STOPPED_UNQUALIFIED_WORKLOAD`.** This record is preserved (`chain.status`, `results/qualify_frozen.json`).
- **Cause.** Every workload criterion Q1–Q7 passed for E1 and E2. The stop came from the renderer validation only: test V3, clause "in-place edit == rebuilt scene", on E2 (per-pixel maximum difference 0.0066 of the image mean at 256 spp against a tolerance of 2e-4). All other tests passed (V1 against Mitsuba's path integrator within 0.6 %, V2, V4–V8).
- **Diagnosis** (`diag_v3.py`, `results/amend1/v3_diagnosis.json`). E2's edit rotates box B. The in-place vertices differ from the rebuilt scene's by 6e-8 m (one float32 unit in the last place). That changes one path sample in 2 of 65,536 pixels: the difference scales as 1 / spp (0.0066 at 256 spp, 0.0017 at 1,024 spp). Frame-level relative difference 9e-8; relative L1 1.1e-7; state rays identical. E1 (translation only) has no differing pixel.
- **Scope.** Reference renders, oracle fields and ECRO renders build fresh scenes. In-place edits are used by the training-data generator and the economics stage only.

## Amendment 1 (post hoc, labelled; `experiments/ecro86/protocol/amendment1.json`)

- **Change.** V3's in-place clause is evaluated as: vertices within 1e-6 m, frame-level relative difference ≤ 1e-5, relative L1 ≤ 1e-5, fraction of pixels differing by more than 2e-4 × mean ≤ 1e-3, identical state rays. The frozen tolerance was a per-pixel maximum written from a translation-only dev check; it cannot hold when float32 vertex rounding changes a single stochastic sample.
- **Not changed.** Every research threshold, the scene, materials, masks, representation, training setting, and all other tests.
- **Execution.** `RESUME=1 run86.sh` reuses the completed Stage 0 renders, re-evaluates the admission with the amended V3 and continues with Stages 1–4 as frozen.
