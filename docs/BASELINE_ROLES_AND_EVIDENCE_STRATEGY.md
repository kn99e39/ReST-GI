## Mandatory baseline terminology and comparison gate

**Binding research decision (user reaffirmed 2026-11-02):** `Full Recompute` in the **external neural-GI comparison** means **rebuilding/retraining/refitting the neural representation for the new scene configuration when that representation is actually invalidated**. For 8DNA this is a native T1 asset re-training/rebuild (including required post-edit data and checkpoint preparation), **not** running Cycles at T1. This is the user-defined contract already measured historically in [WL27](worklogs/27_WORKLOG_NEURAL_FULL_RECOMPUTE_COST_RNA_8DNA.md). Use the unambiguous term **Neural Full Recompute** in new prompts, protocols, reports, and reviews.

Three distinct comparisons must never be silently conflated:
1. **Neural Full Recompute vs ReST-GI Selective Update:** after an edit that truly invalidates a neural model's transport, compare the native T1 representation recovery lifecycle and quality-matched output against ReST-GI's edit-to-valid-output lifecycle. Declare from-scratch versus genuinely supported warm-start policies. Count reference/data generation, training or refit, validation/checkpoint/export, scene preparation and final inference, separating one-time and per-edit costs and reporting convergence/recovery.
2. **Native Reuse vs ReST-GI Selective Update:** if the external baseline's original learned representation remains physically valid at T1, compare its native no-retraining behavior; do **not** label it Full Recompute and do not impose needless retraining to manufacture an advantage. An independent 8DNA intrinsic slab asset moved rigidly above an external floor can be such a case; whether retraining is required must be established from the baseline's actual representation ownership and the edited dependency, not assumed from scene motion.
3. **Path-traced Full-scene Re-render vs ReST-GI:** Cycles or Mitsuba can provide physical ground truth and an optional direct rendering cost comparator. Its spp-dependent T1 rendering time is **not** the time to rebuild a neural learned representation. Numerical superiority to that path tracer does **not** establish superiority to Neural Full Recompute.

**Mandatory gate before authoring/approving any comparative Agent prompt:** name the precise owned learned state for each arm; show whether T0→T1 actually invalidates it; specify whether native reuse, cold rebuild, supported refit, or within-method state F/S is under test; fix same physically valid workload and T1 quality acceptance; declare timing start/end for data, training, checkpoint, preparation, update and inference; and preserve independent path-traced ground truth. If a neural recovery comparison cannot be physically and operationally aligned, report it **UNMEASURED**, not replace the neural comparator with Cycles. Do not infer that a method needs retraining merely because the overall scene geometry changed. No new experiment is authorised by this document.

**Correction of WL69–70 interpretation (historical protocols and Worklogs unchanged):** The WL69 protocol incorrectly labels its `CY` arm as `Quality-matched Full Recompute`; **it is a Cycles path-tracing re-render**. Its `D8` arm trains 8DNA once on a slab asset and applies the rigid T1 instance transform **without re-training**. WL69 established a shared physical workload and quality measurements, **not** Neural Full Recompute cost. WL70's valid 24.60→2.09 s (11.8×) improvement is a within-ReST-GI first-order sun-state reuse result; its ~2.09 vs 2.40 s comparator is likewise **Cycles re-render time, not 8DNA neural rebuild time**. The WL69 Outcome B and WL70 Outcome A remain their own frozen-protocol verdicts **only**, not evidence of a Neural Full Recompute superiority result. Any previously stated external **Neural Full Recompute advantage on C1 is WITHDRAWN / UNMEASURED**. Preserve the raw measurements and historical terminology in [WL69](worklogs/69_WORKLOG_COMMON_WORKLOAD_EXTERNAL_BASELINE.md) and [WL70](worklogs/70_WORKLOG_SUN_STATE_REUSE_C1.md); never rewrite those records to disguise this contract error.

**External comparison record (WL67).** Comparability classes: physically aligned (same geometry, materials, lighting, cameras and physical reference, native semantics, locally measured timing), partially aligned (timing inherited from other hardware or a declared subset only), not comparable. On the WL22 teaset (aligned for quality, cross-machine reference check passed; neural timings inherited from the RTX 5080), 64-spp Mitsuba path tracing (0.5 s) beats frozen and refitted RNA/8DNA, and ReST-GI has no rough-conductor K. RNA and 8DNA cannot run W1 under native semantics (hemisphere training views never see the gap or bottom faces; native 32-bounce / direct-clamp settings bias the medium by up to −83%; Mitsuba volume boundary-layer bias). Superiority classes A–D and the full table are in the [Worklog](worklogs/67_WORKLOG_BASELINE_SUPERIORITY_EVIDENCE.md).

**Common-workload record (WL69).** The first physically aligned workload shared by ReST-GI and an external neural baseline is the WL55 floor fixture (homogeneous medium slab over a Lambert floor, rigid 1 cm → 4 mm move). The released 8DNA runs there natively: one trained slab asset is moved by its instance transform, and Mitsuba volpath passes the contract at 8DNA's native units. RNA is blocked by its position-AOV semantics at transparent medium boundaries; Neural Radiosity has no medium transport. Under the WL55 rule, ReST-GI CAK passes 12/12 on both states, while 8DNA fails at every spp for both seeds (3–7/12; up to −20 % blue on the medium faces, static bias), so 8DNA is ineligible for superiority claims. Cycles **path-traced re-render** matches CAK's quality at 16 spp in 2.4 s, against a 24 s CAK update (dominated by the Monte Carlo sun term). WL69's frozen protocol assigns outcome B, but this is **not** a Neural Full Recompute comparison. Details and costs are in the [Worklog](worklogs/69_WORKLOG_COMMON_WORKLOAD_EXTERNAL_BASELINE.md).

**Validated state reuse (WL70).** Reusing state in a timed comparison counts as a valid update only with an explicit dependency contract, physical identity measured against independent replicas, and invalidation controls. On C1, first-order sun reuse brings the CAK update to 2.09 s against Cycles' 2.40 s **path-traced re-render** at matched quality (16 spp). This comparison does not measure neural retraining/rebuild. The margin lies within the Cycles timing definition (Blender-internal time 2.05–2.09 s) and is reported as fragile. Cycles at the bare contract (1 spp, 1.2 s) remains cheaper. [Worklog](worklogs/70_WORKLOG_SUN_STATE_REUSE_C1.md).

## Reference validity and comparison units

A credible baseline and independently validated target semantics precede candidate judgment. Freeze the scene/material regime, aggregation, checkpoint rule and noise/convergence controls prospectively. Report a baseline limitation separately from a candidate limitation; never repair a failed gate by changing its threshold or evaluation set after results. [Baseline attribution](worklogs/33_WORKLOG_RNA_STATIC_BASELINE_ROOT_CAUSE_ATTRIBUTION.md) and [qualification](worklogs/35_WORKLOG_RNA_REFERENCE_REPRODUCTION_LEGO.md) own the historical measurements.

Label every comparison with its renderer, build/device, machine, sample/clamping/filter regime and spatial/angular interface. Historical `hist` and corrected `r1` references are different regimes; original RNA/Surface-Owned/SPT-1 scores cannot silently be relabelled as r1. [Regime provenance](worklogs/42_WORKLOG_FNTO_VALIDITY_RESTORATION_R1.md).

Cycles BSSRDF vertex semantics cannot supervise a single vertex-independent recursively composed K without an explicit contract change. Preserve the homogeneous-volume reference distinction and the recorded renderer precision/film limitations. [Semantics attribution](worklogs/51_WORKLOG_FNTO_TRANSPORT_OWNERSHIP.md), [reference controls](worklogs/53_WORKLOG_PHYSICAL_INTRINSIC_TRANSPORT_REFERENCE.md).

Compare compatible area/port integrals and visibility masks. Point-sampled oblique boundary images require pixel convergence; partially visible ports and cameras starting inside a medium need separate validity handling. Resolve near-contact structure on both K and C. Include scattered and ballistic camera contributions exactly once. Diagnostic continuous oracles and fine-cell solutions are controls, not substitutes for an independent physical reference. [Spatial controls](worklogs/54_WORKLOG_EXPLICIT_C_SPATIAL_FIDELITY.md), [readout attribution](worklogs/55_WORKLOG_SPATIAL_INTRINSIC_K_INTERFACE.md), [amended masks](worklogs/56_WORKLOG_EXACT_SELECTIVE_K_RECOMPUTE.md).

## Controlled comparisons and metrics

- Predeclare geometry, materials, illumination, cameras, splits, coordinate/correspondence conventions, target semantics, seeds, precision, training/checkpoint rules and acceptance thresholds in the owning protocol. Keep held-out edits out of fitting and selection.
- Isolate the declared mechanism. For Surface-Owned comparisons change the ownership boundary and necessary composition only; for persistent-state F/S use the same architecture and rule checkpoint. Report capacity confounds rather than hiding them.
- Evaluate unseen spatial and directional incident fields and all-boundary sender outputs. Zero/linearity/nonnegativity/passivity/cache tests are necessary but do not establish angular, deformation or coupled fidelity.
- Report absolute operator/image quality separately from full/selective equivalence, signed change and stationary-receiver recovery. Include HDR error, glossy/high-frequency, shadow/material and boundary/interior regions; declare aggregation and uncertainty.
- Track geometry-dirty versus transport-dirty support. For complete-unit reconstruction report complete learned-state ownership, unchanged parameter identity, declared output-influence/composition support and radiometric validity separately. Oracle dirty sets require declared noise, split, coverage and cost.
- Include selection/dependency maintenance, geometry preparation, normalization, cache build, K/C application, solve, readout, loading/checkpoint traffic, warm/cold lifecycle time and unique memory. Separate offline reference generation/training from runtime update/query costs; report hardware contention and convergence. Unit counts and parameter counts do not prove speedups.
- Preserve reviewer-visible ownership/seam maps, error and changed/stationary/unaffected-region views where applicable, with provenance in `results/evaluation/<worklog>/`. Scripts, gates and detailed metrics remain in their owning protocol/Worklog/results.

The original protocol owns its numerical gates. Use a valid spectral-radius estimator (A² iteration or Arnoldi for sign-paired modes), and distinguish numerical convergence from physical correctness; [estimator audit](worklogs/54_WORKLOG_EXPLICIT_C_SPATIAL_FIDELITY.md).

## Future selection and evidence breadth

A physically interpretable algorithmic spatial/directional hierarchy is a candidate-discovery preference, not an adopted transport solver. Search hierarchy, transport dependency graph, parameter ownership and invalidation scoring are distinct. Normal similarity or local distance alone cannot guarantee coverage, especially with third-party occlusion and nonlocal glossy/multi-bounce transport.

Before any separately authorised learned-selector test, establish physical-oracle candidate coverage. Compare algorithm-only and learned scoring on identical candidates, labels and held-out scenes. Report radiometrically weighted false negatives, false-positive work and total discovery/inference/recompute cost. A missed candidate is irrecoverable downstream. No runtime oracle-label leakage; any fallback and tolerance must be prospective.

One asset, material family, edit or candidate failure cannot establish project-level generality. Revisit rigid, local deformation, global bending/twisting and self-contact at major decisions; test sequentially. Prior art and novelty constraints are owned by the [research topic](RESEARCH_CENTRIC_TOPIC.md).

## Interpretation and stopping

Stop on invalid reference/baseline semantics, lost required information, failed ownership/correspondence, fundamental composition failure or broken controls. Attribute baseline, candidate, ownership, optimisation/capacity, partition, supervision/correspondence and composition causes before interpreting results. No rescue heuristic, threshold revision, automatic successor or adoption follows from a result.
