## Independent research synthesis (blind track; only the neutral brief was read)

### 1. A finding all five specialists reached independently
All five specialists (physics, neural, geometry, systems, critic) derived the same structural fact without seeing each other. I re-derived it, and I agree.

**Assumptions (FACT, from the brief):** the medium is homogeneous, the phase function is isotropic, the boundary is index-matched with escape only, and σ_t is the same in all channels.

**DERIVATION:** the collided operator then factorises exactly into three parts, which is the Peierls / collision-probability form:
- an exact **first-flight** factor F_in(i,a → x), a geometric ray integral;
- a **scalar, direction-free, source-independent** collision resolvent G_c = (I − ω_c W)^{-1}, where W is the geometric flight kernel and ω_c is the only quantity that differs between channels;
- an exact **last-flight escape** factor Φout(x → j,o), a geometric ray integral.

**Consequences:**
- **(DERIVATION)** Every incoming-bin dependence, and therefore all of `con_*`, enters only through F_in. Every outgoing-bin dependence enters only through Φout. Nothing angular remains to be learned.
- **(DERIVATION)** Σ_x F_in = d(i,a), so the collided budget and the separate ballistic operator B agree by construction.
- **(INFERENCE)** This is consistent with the one measured hint in the brief: the first-interaction anchoring was exact, and its noise-free angular floor is ≈ 0.07. The learned *angular redistribution* in that approach was trying to learn something that, under isotropic scattering, is the exact escape geometry Φout.

The specialists also agree, with my independent check (DERIVATION, diffusion approximation as a scale guide), on these numbers:
- **Diffusion lengths:** L ≈ 18.3 / 5.8 / 1.8 mm (R/G/B). The ordering is consistent with the measured 5.5 / 3.2 / 0.1 % of energy beyond 128 ports.
- **Slowest slab mode:** λ ≈ 0.975, giving a red resolvent gain 1/(1 − ωλ) ≈ 38 and about 260 plain Neumann iterations to reach 1e-3.
- **Context classes at rest:** about 52, combining the exact D4h group with the measured translation equivalence beyond about 18.75 mm. S16-R supplies at most about 13 symmetry-distinct rest sources, so most BND_FAR classes (corners, vertical edges, side rims) are plausibly **unsupervised classes**. This coverage claim is INFERENCE, because the exact S16-R positions are not in the brief.

**INFERENCE shared by all five:**
- The measured family-level outcome (fits supervised sources, fails on independent BND_FAR) fits a model in which source identity or context is passed through a learned function. Transfer then becomes extrapolation in context space.
- No representation that keeps a learned source→response map can transfer to a context class that has no supervised member, unless built-in structure supplies that class.
- The causal share of each factor (data volume, invariance, optimisation, angular basis) is **not measured** in the brief.

### 2. Points of agreement
- **Dense K is infeasible.** 4.5e10 entries, about 181 GB in fp32. K must be applied as an operator.
- **Learning must never see the source.** A new source should enter only as an exactly computed vector (F_in).
- **Exact D4h equivariance comes from the construction, not from a penalty.** Use a symmetric lattice and orbit-symmetrised quadrature.
- **The result is domain-specific.** It depends on isotropic phase, index matching and homogeneity. With anisotropic g, a Fresnel boundary or heterogeneity, the scalar middle becomes angular or non-convolutional. None of the candidates carries its generalisation argument outside this physics unchanged.
- **This largely dissolves the "learned K" premise for this test domain.** Whether that is acceptable is a research-owner decision, not a technical one.
- **Validate conventions first.** Before any interpretation, check the port/bin and radiance-weighting conventions: single-scatter T0 against a reference tally, and the half-space H-function. The q = 1,024 confound in the brief is a direct precedent for this kind of quadrature or convention trap.
- **Discretisation error concentrates where the learned operators failed.** It is largest at boundary layers, edges, corners and grazing bins. So the falsification test must check whether BND_FAR error *converges under refinement*. Passing on average is not enough.

### 3. Disagreements and unresolved technical points
- **Discretisation.** Options range from a uniform 0.5 mm D4h-symmetric lattice (8.0e5 cells; W as a masked FFT convolution, exact for a convex body) to graded meshes (3e4–1.1e5 cells; sparse W). The uniform lattice gives exact equivariance and FFT speed. The graded mesh resolves the boundary layer more cheaply but loses the FFT. Unresolved; it needs the refinement study.
- **Solver, linearity and positivity.**
  - The neural specialist proposed CG. The geometry specialist correctly pointed out that **truncated Krylov iterates depend on the right-hand side, so they are not exactly linear**.
  - Exactly linear options are a fixed linear iteration (fixed sweeps plus a fixed coarse direct inverse) or a converged solve.
  - DERIVATION (mine): Neumann residuals from zero, r_k = (ωW)^{k+1}q, are ≥ 0. A Galerkin coarse correction with non-negative restriction/prolongation and a sub-stochastic coarse operator is therefore ≥ 0. It can still *overshoot*, so the upper passivity bound is not guaranteed during truncated two-grid iteration. It holds only at convergence (HYPOTHESIS: small; measure it).
- **Cost.** Specialist estimates for one K application range from 5 to 150 ms. They disagree mainly on grid size and on DSA effectiveness at h ≈ 0.5 mfp. All agree it is slower per application than the ms-scale learned operator. All agree that folding the collision densities into the outer K∘C solve as joint unknowns (one W application per outer iteration) is the natural runtime form. Whether this beats "a few seconds" of path-traced re-rendering is **unmeasured**.
- **What to store.**
  - Solve at runtime (C1 below).
  - Learn a continuous bounded Green's function (C2).
  - Tabulate context-class responses (C3).
  - Precompute a port-space near-field plus slow-mode operator (C4).

  These are genuinely different representations of the same factorised physics, with different invariant guarantees, rebuild costs and failure modes. I keep them separate.
- **Context-equivalence cutoff.** It was measured only on the near field and fine16 contrast, at one pair. The red diffusion length (18.3 mm) is close to the 18.75 mm cutoff, so red far-field equivalence is the weakest assumption behind C3. The geometry, neural and critic specialists all flagged this.

### 4. My own independent additions (marked as mine in the specs)
- **(a) Domain monotonicity (DERIVATION).** With an escaping boundary, the bounded-medium resolvent is a path sum over a subset of the infinite-medium paths, all with non-negative weights. So 0 ≤ G_Ω(x,y) ≤ G∞(x,y). A learned bounded Green's function can therefore be written as G∞·τ with τ ∈ [0,1], which is non-negative by construction (used in C2).
- **(b) Exact energy recalibration with a geometry-only scale (DERIVATION).**
  - One adjoint solve per channel per geometry, w* = ω(I − ωWᵀ)^{-1}e, gives the exact collided output total of every source column as F_inᵀw*.
  - Any approximate representation (C2, C3, C4) can be rescaled per column by an input-independent factor.
  - This keeps linearity and makes the per-bin energy totals exact.
- **(c) In-plane translation invariance of the slab Green's function (DERIVATION).** For the infinite slab, G_slab depends only on (z_x, z_y, ρ). The dominant top/bottom bounded problem is therefore an exact 3-variable function. Only the sides, edges, corners and dents need corrections.
- **(d) Equivariant modal truncation (DERIVATION).** A truncated modal representation is exactly D4h-equivariant only if it keeps whole degenerate eigenspaces, because the irrep projectors commute with the group.

### 5. What no candidate establishes
- No candidate's accuracy at the gates is known. Every claim of "transfer by construction" is conditional on discretisation convergence near edges and corners, and that is a HYPOTHESIS.
- No candidate is shown to be cheaper than re-rendering.
- Dent signed-change accuracy is a HYPOTHESIS for all of them. Bias from different discretisations at rest and when dented may not cancel.
- The brief does not say whether the MC reference packets contain collision-order tallies. Checking the single-scatter convention may need a short new MC run, or a deterministic-versus-MC single-scatter comparison.

### 6. Shared first experiment (mechanism-level, before any candidate-specific test)
**Bin-averaged-F_in control.** Replace F_in by its mean over incoming bins while keeping everything else exact. The derivation predicts:
- `con_*` collapses to about 0;
- `abs_*` barely moves.

If the exact F_in does not reproduce the reference contrast, then the claim that "all incoming-direction dependence enters through the first collision" is wrong for this reference or contract. Every candidate below would then lose its central argument together.

## Candidate C1: CP-K: explicit collision-probability intrinsic operator (exact flights + solved scalar resolvent)

**Central mechanism.** K is not learned but computed. The exact Peierls factorisation for a homogeneous, isotropic-scattering, index-matched medium is discretised: an exact first-flight deposit F_in, an exact last-flight escape Φout, and a scalar, channel-free geometric flight kernel W whose resolvent (I − ω_c W)^{-1} is solved at runtime, for example by a masked FFT convolution with diffusion-synthetic acceleration, or jointly with the outer K∘C solve. Single scatter is split off and integrated exactly at point quadrature. Generalisation to unseen sources holds by construction, because no source-indexed quantity is ever fitted. The only error is discretisation and solver error, which can be checked by refinement without references. This is the convergent preference of all five specialists (physics A, neural A, geometry M1, systems A, critic M1).

### Physical operator

**Quantities.**
- q = F_in·u is the first-collision density for incident packet u.
- ψ = (I − ω_c W)^{-1} q is the collision density.
- The output is ω_c Φout ψ.

**Split form.** The single-scatter term is separated (critic's proposal):

`K_c = ω_c S1 + ω_c² Φout W (I − ω_c W)^{-1} F_in`, with `S1 = Φout∘F_in`.

- S1 is evaluated at fine point quadrature, not cell-averaged: a first-collision-source method.
- Cells carry only the smoother multiply-scattered remainder.

**Factors** (cells x, y; ports i, j; bins a, o):
- `F_in[x; i,a]` = ∫_port ∫_bin (contract weighting) ∫ σ_t e^{−σ_t s} 1[r(s)∈x] ds. It uses the *same rays as B*, so Σ_x F_in = d(i,a) holds to float precision.
- `W[y,x]` = probability that an isotropic emission uniform in x has its next collision in y. Only in-medium chords count; a chord through the dent cavity is an exit (C's re-entry).
- `Φout[j,o; y]` = probability that an isotropic emission in y escapes uncollided through port j into bin o, tallied with the reference's radiance-weighted power convention.
- **Partition of unity per cell, by construction** (each sampled emission either collides in some cell or exits): 1ᵀW + 1ᵀΦout = 1ᵀ.

### Explicit information
- F_in, W, Φout and the in-medium visibility, all from the current mesh and port frames.
- The albedos ω_c and σ_t.
- B and d, unchanged.

### Learned information
None is required. Two optional parts:
- **(a) Conservative local closure ΔW** on boundary-cut cells (neural A, geometry M1). About 1e3 parameters, inputs E(3)-invariant: signed distances to up to 3 nearby planes, dihedral type, dent curvature. It is parameterised as a symmetric, non-negative redistribution among nearby target cells with row sums held fixed. It is trained on deterministic fine-grid canonical patches (half-space, slab, 90° wedge, trihedral corner, dent family), never on source packets.
- **(b) A learned preconditioner or prolongation** (systems A). It affects only speed; at convergence the answer is the discretised physics.

### Source conditioning
The source is only the right-hand side F_in[:, i, a], an exact ray integral. It never enters a learned function.

### Geometry conditioning
- The medium mask and the port frames.
- In-medium chord visibility. For a convex body the masked convolution is exact (DERIVATION: a convex set contains all its chords). For a dent, a sparse near-field chord correction covers pairs within about 6 mm of the dent surface. Longer blocked chords carry less than about e^{-6}/(4πr²) (INFERENCE).

### Angular and spatial representation
- **Angle:** none inside the medium (DERIVATION: isotropic scattering). Angle appears only as the integration domains of the analytic boundary flights. These are integrated with deterministic, stratified, orbit-symmetrised quadrature, checked for convergence. This addresses the q = 1,024 lesson.
- **Space, two implementation options:**
  - (U) Uniform D4h-symmetric lattice, h = 0.5 mm: 200×200×20 = 8.0e5 cells. W is applied by a masked FFT convolution on a 400×400×40 zero-padded grid (6.4e6 points), with the self-cell and near-field kernel integrated analytically.
  - (G) Graded mesh: lateral 1.25 mm, refined to 0.25 mm near side faces, depth layers 0.25→1.5 mm. About 3e4–1.1e5 cells, with sparse W.
- **Optional linear-in-cell source** for boundary-layer accuracy. Its benefit is a HYPOTHESIS.

### Transformation behaviour
- **Exact rest symmetries.** With a symmetric lattice and symmetrised quadrature, g acts by permutation on cells, ports and bins, so K = P_g K Q_gᵀ to float precision (DERIVATION).
- **Rigid motion:** invariant in the body frame; no rebuild.
- **Dent:**
  - Re-mask the cells.
  - Recompute the F_in / Φout rows for about 450 ports within about 5 mfp of the dent (dent radius ≤ 25 mm + 5 mm: π·30²/6.25 ≈ 450).
  - Recompute the dent chord correction.
  - The FFT kernel spectrum is unchanged because the medium is homogeneous.
- Frames on the dented pose may follow any convention, because bins are integrated in whatever frame the contract defines.

### Information preservation
- No compression of source information.
- The collided budget is exact.
- The only losses are spatial discretisation (flat or linear source per cell), quadrature and solver tolerance, all of which can be refined.

### Physical invariants and how they are guaranteed (DERIVATION unless noted)
- **Linearity and zero-in / zero-out:** a product of fixed linear maps, *provided* the solve is a fixed linear iteration (fixed sweep count plus a fixed coarse direct inverse) or converged. Truncated CG / Krylov is right-hand-side dependent and **not** exactly linear.
- **Non-negativity:** all factors are ≥ 0. Neumann partial sums are ≥ 0 and increase monotonically. Neumann residuals (ωW)^{k+1}q are ≥ 0, so a Galerkin coarse correction with non-negative R/P and a sub-stochastic coarse operator is ≥ 0. Overshoot is possible (HYPOTHESIS: small).
- **Passivity:** from 1ᵀψ = 1ᵀq + ω(1ᵀψ − eᵀψ), the total output is ω eᵀψ = d − (1−ω)1ᵀψ ≤ d(i,a). This is exact at convergence. A truncated Neumann iteration only underestimates; a two-grid iteration may overshoot slightly (measure it).
- **Correspondence:** every index is a computed physical integral.
- **Ownership:** only in-medium chords; first exit terminates.
- **Reciprocity:** V_x W[x,y] = V_y W[y,x] analytically. Φout is the reverse-beam F_in up to cosine, area and solid-angle factors, so reciprocity can be enforced by computing each pair once.

### Generalisation mechanism
- Nothing is indexed by a source or a context.
- A corner source differs from a training source only in its exact F_in column.
- The corner's boundary-layer physics is in W and Φout, an exact kernel restricted to the true geometry.
- The residual error does not depend on which sources were supervised. Whether it is small at corners is a HYPOTHESIS, and it is the central falsifier.

### Training supervision
- None for the operator.
- The MC references (replicas 4–7) are used only for validation.
- If used, ΔW is trained on deterministic canonical-patch solves.

### Computational complexity (ESTIMATES)
- **State:** 184,320 inputs / 737,280 outputs.
- **(U):**
  - F_in: about 61,440 beams × ~400 cells ≈ 2.5e7 nnz (~0.2 GB).
  - Φout: about 245,760 reverse beams × ~400 ≈ 1e8 nnz (~0.6–1 GB), or computed on the fly.
  - One W application: ~0.3–1 ms per channel.
  - Red needs about 260 plain Neumann iterations to reach 1e-3 (λ ≈ 0.975, gain ≈ 38); two-grid / DSA needs about 10–20 (UNVERIFIED at h ≈ 0.5 mfp).
  - Cold K application: about 5–40 ms for RGB.
  - Inside the GI solve, with collision densities as joint unknowns: about one W application per outer iteration.
  - Memory: about 1–3 GB.
- **(G):**
  - W ≈ 5e7 nnz (~0.4 GB).
  - Diffusion-estimated condition numbers 38 / 28 / 8 (R/G/B), so about 25 / 21 / 11 iterations of a symmetrised solver.
  - About 10–30 ms per application.
- **Dent rebuild:** about 0.05–1 s, dominated by the chord correction. At 0.5 mm that is about 1e5 cells × ~2,000 partners ≈ 2e8 nnz (1.6 GB); with 1 mm cells near the dent it is much smaller.
- **Compared with the brief:** about 5–30× slower per application than the ms-scale learned operator, and a similar rebuild time.

### Potential failure cases
- **Flat-source collision-probability error concentrated in 3-D boundary layers** at edges and corners and in grazing bins: exactly the BND_FAR strata. Convergence there may need h ≤ 0.25 mm, which is 1.6–3.2e6 cells (uniform) or breaks the FFT (graded).
- **Red amplifies spatial redistribution error** (gain ≈ 38). Conservation is exact; redistribution is not.
- **Convention mismatch** of the port/bin or radiance weighting with the reference.
- **Dent:** a sharp rim makes the chord correction non-negligible. Discretisation bias between rest and dent may not cancel in the signed change.
- **Solver:** DSA degrades when cells are not small relative to the mfp; overshoot is possible.
- **Cost:** with 10–30 GI iterations, a solve may take 0.1–1 s, against the few seconds of re-rendering. The margin is unmeasured.
- **Scope:** valid only for isotropic, homogeneous, index-matched media. Cell count scales as volume/h³.

### Plausible novel contribution (INFERENCE; modest)
The numerics are classical. What may be new is their use as the reusable intrinsic K of a dynamic GI split, with all of these together:
- an exact discrete passivity identity at a port/bin first-exit contract;
- exact ballistic complementarity (Σ F_in = d);
- exact D4h equivariance;
- local rebuild under surface deformation;
- joint collision-density unknowns with explicit C.

The research consequence is that the unseen-source problem becomes a discretisation-convergence problem.

## Candidate C2: SBG: learned scalar bounded-medium Green's function between exact flights (chart-space supervision)

**Central mechanism.** Keep the exact flights F_in and Φout, and the exact single-scatter term. Replace the iterative resolvent by a learned, non-iterative, source-independent scalar Green's function of the bounded medium. It is written as the analytic infinite-medium resolvent G∞(|x−y|; ω) times a bounded transmission factor τ_θ ∈ [0,1], which depends only on invariant local geometry descriptors of x and y. Domain monotonicity makes τ ∈ [0,1] physically exact rather than heuristic. The in-plane-invariant slab part is an exact 3-variable function, and the side, edge, corner and dent corrections are learned from canonical geometric contexts (charts), not from source packets. Learning is moved from source space, where only about 13 points are available, to chart space, which can be covered exhaustively and without noise. Sources from systems B, critic M2 and neural A's closure idea; τ-monotonicity and the adjoint energy recalibration are my own additions.

### Physical operator

`K_c = ω_c S1 + ω_c² Φout Ĝ_c F_in`

`Ĝ_c(x,y) = vis_Ω(x,y) · G∞_c(|x−y|) · τ_θ(φ(x), φ(y), Δ_can(x,y); ω_c)`

- **G∞_c** is the infinite-medium post-first-collision resolvent kernel, W(I−ωW)^{-1} in free space. DERIVATION: in Fourier space it is λ(k)/(1 − ωλ(k)) with λ(k) = arctan(k/σ_t)/(k/σ_t). It is tabulated once as a 1-D radial function per ω.
- **Structure (my DERIVATION, extending systems B):**
  - For the infinite slab, G_slab(x,y) depends only on (z_x, z_y, ρ), so τ_slab(z_x, z_y, ρ) is an exact 3-variable function.
  - Sides, vertical edges, corners and the dent add multiplicative factors τ_side, τ_edge, τ_corner and τ_dent.
  - The product composition is a HYPOTHESIS: exact image rules hold only for diffusion with extrapolated boundaries.
- **Physical constraint (my DERIVATION, domain monotonicity).** With an escaping boundary, the paths of Ω are a subset of the paths of ℝ³, all with non-negative weights. Therefore 0 ≤ G_Ω ≤ G∞ pointwise, and τ ∈ [0,1] is exact physics, not a clamp.

### Explicit information
- F_in, Φout and S1 (exact, as in C1).
- G∞ (analytic) and in-medium visibility vis_Ω (explicit, including the dent cavity).
- Invariant chart descriptors φ: signed distances to up to 3 nearby boundary planes or the dent surface, dihedral / trihedral type, dent depth / radius / local curvature, in a gauge-fixed canonical frame.

### Learned information
- **τ_θ only.** It is scalar and symmetric in (x,y) by symmetric feature construction, which gives reciprocity.
- **Form:** a table plus a small MLP, about 1e3–1e5 parameters, output through a sigmoid into [0,1].
- **Channel:** either one network per channel or ω as an input (albedo-generic).
- **Never** takes source or receiver port identity, or absolute coordinates.

### Source conditioning
The source is only F_in, which is exact. Unseen sources are not out of distribution for the learned part.

### Geometry conditioning
- Through φ (local charts) and vis_Ω.
- At rest the chart types are finite: slab interior, side face, vertical edge, top/bottom edge, trihedral corner.
- Dents add a low-dimensional curvature family.

### Angular and spatial representation
- **Angle:** only in the exact flights. G and τ are scalar.
- **Space:** collision sites on the C1 lattice (0.5 mm), or on a graded mesh.
- **Application layout:**
  - G∞ part: one masked 3-D FFT.
  - Slab part: per-face 2-D FFTs in-plane with depth-layer coupling (about 20 source depth layers → 40 2-D FFTs of 400×400, comparable to one 3-D FFT).
  - Edge / corner / dent factors: sparse local operators.

### Transformation behaviour
- **D4h:** exact, because φ is invariant and the lattice and quadrature are symmetric.
- **Rigid motion:** invariant.
- **Dent:** recompute φ, vis_Ω and the local flights. τ_dent is evaluated, not retrained, within the trained curvature family.
- **Signed changes:** come from the change in geometric inputs to a physically bounded kernel (a HYPOTHESIS on accuracy).

### Information preservation
- Source information is preserved exactly (flights).
- The approximation is confined to one scalar bounded factor.
- **Errors are not iteration-amplified** (DERIVATION): Ĝ is resolvent-level, so a τ error enters the output once. In C1, by contrast, a W error is amplified by up to about 38× in red.

### Physical invariants and how they are guaranteed
- **Linearity, zero-in / zero-out:** structural; a fixed linear map.
- **Non-negativity:** structural (G∞ ≥ 0, τ ∈ [0,1], vis ≥ 0). This holds only for the **product** form. The faster **additive** face-correction form (G∞ − Σ Γ_f) can go negative, so the two forms trade off.
- **Passivity:** not automatic, because the discrete energy identity of C1 needs the exact resolvent. Two remedies, both my DERIVATION:
  - (i) Geometry-only per-column scaling using the approximate adjoint importance w = Ĝᵀ Φoutᵀ 1: each column total is computable as ω² F_inᵀw + ω·colsum(S1), and scaling keeps linearity.
  - (ii) Exact recalibration with one C1 adjoint solve per channel per geometry, w* = ω(I − ωWᵀ)^{-1}e, which makes the per-bin energy totals exact.
- **Reciprocity:** structural if τ is symmetric.
- **Ownership:** vis_Ω excludes air chords.
- **Ballistic complementarity:** exact through F_in.

### Generalisation mechanism
The learned function lives in chart space, not source space.
- Every boundary context of the rest box decomposes into a finite set of canonical charts. This rests on the measured locality FACT: 99–99.9 % of boundary norm within 5 mm, and second-boundary irrelevance at 18.75 mm (near field).
- Those charts are supervised **exhaustively**, independently of where the MC sources were.
- A corner source needs τ_corner, which is supervised from corner-chart solves, not from corner sources.

### Training supervision
The options are not source packets:
- (a) Deterministic C1 solves on canonical patches (half-space, 10-mfp slab, 90° side wedge, trihedral corner, dent family). These are noise-free and unlimited, but make C2 depend on C1 being validated first.
- (b) MC volume point-source collision-density tallies in the same canonical patches. These are denser per photon than exit tensors (critic's suggestion), and independent of C1.
- (c) Optionally, least-squares refinement through the exact F_in / Φout on the existing S16-R packets. These are used only as a check or a light fine-tune, with the INT / BND populations held out.

### Computational complexity (ESTIMATES)
- **Flights:** as in C1 (F_in ≈ 2.5e7 nnz; Φout ≈ 1e8 nnz or on the fly).
- **Ĝ application, single pass, no iteration:**
  - 3-D FFT G∞: ~1 ms per channel.
  - Slab depth-coupled 2-D FFTs: ~1 ms per channel.
  - Local edge / corner / dent sparse terms: ~1e7–1e8 nnz.
  - Total about 3–10 ms for RGB.
- **Parameters:** about 1e4–1e5.
- **Memory:** about 1–2 GB.
- **Dent rebuild:** features + local flights + chord visibility ≈ 0.05–0.3 s. Optional exact adjoint recalibration adds about 3 C1 solves, ≈ 0.1–0.5 s.
- **Training:** the canonical-chart dataset is about 10²–10³ patch solves (C1) or MC point-source runs. GPU-hours, once per material class (ω-generic if ω is an input).

### Potential failure cases
- The product (or image) composition at edges and corners is not exact in transport. The 3-D boundary layer at a trihedral corner may be poorly captured by a factorised τ.
- The multiplicative τ near the singular diagonal (x≈y) must be handled with analytic near-field integration; a cell-scale error there dominates the near field.
- Dent curvature or rim shapes outside the trained family.
- Visibility inside a strongly non-convex dent is explicit, but τ's features may not capture the dent's shadowing of the multiply scattered field.
- Dependence on C1 as teacher: if C1 fails to converge at corners, option (a) supervision inherits the error. Option (b) avoids this at MC cost.
- With remedy (i), passivity bookkeeping is approximate.
- Domain-specific: isotropic phase, homogeneous, index-matched.

### Closest prior work
(Partly from specialists' memory, not re-verified.)
- Dipole (Jensen et al. 2001), multipole slab images (Donner & Jensen 2005), quantized diffusion (d'Eon & Irving 2011), directional dipole (Frisvad et al. 2014). These are analytic bounded-medium kernels via images in the *diffusion* regime.
- Photon Beam Diffusion (Habel et al. 2013): exact beam × kernel sandwich.
- Chandrasekhar half-space H-function theory: the exact transport half-space solution.
- Vicini, Koltun & Jakob 2019, learned shape-adaptive SSS: local polynomial shape descriptors in a canonical frame, but a stochastic exit sampler, not a passive linear operator.
- Moon, Walter & Marschner 2007: precomputed canonical shell transport.

### Plausible novel contribution (INFERENCE)
A *transport-level*, not diffusion-level, bounded-medium scalar Green's function. It is:
- learned as a monotonicity-bounded transmission factor relative to G∞;
- supervised exhaustively in chart space rather than source space;
- sandwiched between exact flights;
- given exact per-bin energy by a geometry-only adjoint recalibration.

As far as the specialists know, image or face-separable composition for *unsupervised* corner contexts has not been tested in transport.

### Minimal falsification experiment
1. **Exactness check.** Fit τ_slab only (infinite slab). Verify against C1 or MC on slab-interior INT sources that the slab-only Ĝ reaches the gates. A failure falsifies the non-iterative resolvent form itself.
2. **Chart-transfer test.** Fit τ_side and τ_edge on half-space / side-wedge / edge charts **only, with no corner data**. Compose τ_corner by product. Evaluate the BND_FAR corner and vertical-edge strata against the existing MC references, with exact adjoint recalibration on and off.
3. **Falsified if:**
   - corner / vertical-edge con_coarse > 0.25 or abs_coarse > 0.10 while face charts pass (the composition does not transfer); or
   - SBG's error exceeds C1's error at the same discretisation by more than 1.25× on any stratum (the learned bounded kernel is worse than solving).
4. **Dent:** signed-change relative error ≤ 0.35 and correlation ≥ 0.90 on the existing dented pose, with τ_dent trained on a dent family that excludes that exact pose.

Cost: GPU-hours. No new source packets.

## Candidate C3: CCA: gauge-canonical context atlas of port-space responses (+ path-splitting composition for unsupervised corner classes)

**Central mechanism.** The finite rest body has a finite set of source contexts once two things are combined: the exact D4h group, and the measured translation equivalence (second-nearest boundary irrelevant beyond about 18.75 mm). That is about 52 classes: 36 on the top/bottom faces and 16 on the side faces. Store one port-space near-field response table per class in a gauge-canonical frame, and read every port's response through its exact group element, port relabelling and bin permutations. The tables can be supervised directly by existing-pipeline MC (about 3.5 GPU-h per material), with no deterministic solver at all. This makes CCA the only candidate physically independent of the Peierls discretisation. Corner classes can alternatively be composed from edge-class kernels by an exact first-crossing (Dyson-type) path-splitting identity. Prediction becomes complete enumeration (rest) or low-dimensional interpolation (dent), never extrapolation in source space. Sources: neural B, geometry M2 and physics B's context-class resolvent.

### Physical operator

`K[i,a,j,o,c] = ρ_i · A_{κ(i)}[π_{g_i}(a), τ_i(j), π_{g_i}(o), c]   for j ∈ N(i)`
`            + K_far[i,a,j,o,c]   for j ∉ N(i)`

- **κ(i):** context class, (face type, clipped distances k_1 ≤ k_2 to the nearest boundaries in port units, cap 7 → 18.75 mm).
  - Top/bottom: 8·9/2 = 36 classes, unordered because of the diagonal mirror.
  - Sides: {rim row, middle row} × along-face index 0..7 = 16 classes.
  - Total ≈ 52 (DERIVATION, conditional on the equivalence cutoff).
- **g_i ∈ D4h** maps i to its class representative. π_g are the exact bin permutations, already verified exact in the brief. τ_i relabels receivers by g_i plus an in-face translation.
- **N(i):** near window, about 256 receivers on both faces. FACT: 99–99.9 % of boundary-source norm lies within 5 mm, and for interior sources 94.5 % of red energy lies within 128 ports.
- **K_far:** a coarse long-range term. Options: the slab slow-mode tail (C4), a coarse C1 solve, or a single-slab-mode diffusion kernel.
- **ρ_i:** a geometry-only per-column scale that sets the column total to an exact or estimated budget (see passivity).
- **Optional corner composition (neural B, DERIVATION of the identity):**

  `T_corner ≈ (Q⊗P) T_parent − Σ_{z∈Σ_trunc,ω} K[i,a,z,ω] · R_parent[z,ω → j,o]`

  - Split each parent-medium path at its first crossing of a truncation plane that the parent lacks.
  - If the plane's re-crossing kernel has norm < 1, the coupled form (I + X)^{-1} is a convergent series. That is plausible because top and bottom absorb quickly (INFERENCE).
  - The kernel is block-Toeplitz along the edge, so an FFT diagonalises it.

### Explicit information
The class map κ, the gauge elements g_i, the port and bin permutations, the window N, the composition operators, B and d.

### Learned / tabulated information
- The class tables A_κ. These are tabulated MC means or C1 outputs, not fitted networks.
- For dented poses only: an interpolator over port contexts in the dent's influence region (a continuum, about 1e3–2e3 ports). It is a network in a dent-canonical frame, with inputs (radial offset / radius, depth, radius, receiver offset).
- Its angular state is held in a steerable basis (circular harmonics in azimuth × elevation profiles), then projected onto the contract bins by exact precomputed bin-integral matrices. Reason (geometry M2): the 4×4 / 8×8 square-to-disk bins carry only D4, so a rotation about the dent axis does not permute bins.

### Source conditioning
Only through the discrete class index and the exact group element. No continuous source features enter at rest.

### Geometry conditioning
- **Rest:** class map + gauge.
- **Dent:** the canonical dent frame + the interpolator.
- **Rigid motion:** body frame.

### Angular and spatial representation
- Native port × 64-bin tables in the canonical frame. There is no shared angular profile basis, which avoids the measured 1.26–1.41× orientation bias of shared profiles.
- Exact equivariance comes from reading one entry through permutations.

### Transformation behaviour
- **D4h:** exact by construction (DERIVATION).
- **Rigid motion:** invariant.
- **Dent:** interpolation in about 3 dent parameters + local offsets. This reintroduces a coverage question in shape space.

### Information preservation
- **Rest:** lossless up to the class tables' MC noise, the window truncation (≈ 0.1–5.5 % of energy beyond 128 ports, by channel; the far term must carry it) and the equivalence cutoff.
- **Dent:** limited by the interpolator.

### Physical invariants and how they are guaranteed
- **Linearity, zero-in / zero-out:** structural.
- **Non-negativity:** MC tallies are ≥ 0, and C1 outputs are ≥ 0. K_far must be chosen non-negative.
- **Passivity:** each table column is an estimate of a passive operator. Σ ≤ d holds up to noise and window truncation. Enforce it with the geometry-only scale ρ_i: the exact budget from a C1 adjoint (C2 remedy ii) or the MC column total. This keeps linearity.
- **Correspondence:** exact permutations.
- **Ownership:** inherited from the supervising reference.
- **Reciprocity:** not guaranteed. It can be imposed by symmetrising A with its reverse-bin transpose where both ports belong to stored classes.

### Generalisation mechanism
- **At rest there is no unseen class.** Every port maps to one of about 52 tabulated classes.
- "Transfer" rests on two measured or exact facts: the exact group, and the translation equivalence (3a: near field 0.002 against noise 0.002; fine16 contrast 0.005).
- Corners can be enumerated, or composed, which is the falsifiable path-splitting claim.
- **Dent:** interpolation over a densely sampled synthetic family generated by C1 or C2.

### Training supervision
- **Option (MC):** one source per class. 52 × 4 GPU-min ≈ 3.5 GPU-h per material and thickness. Use replicas 0–3 for the table and 4–7 to validate. Multiplexing several sources per packet at > 30 mm separation could cut packets 2–3× (INFERENCE, neural B).
- **Option (C1):** deterministic and noise-free.
- **Dent interpolator:** C1/C2-generated data. Per-dent MC is infeasible: about 1e3–2e3 ports × 4 GPU-min ≈ 70–130 GPU-h per dent.

### Computational complexity
- **Rest tables:** 52 × 16 × 256 × 64 × 3 × 4 B ≈ 164 MB (DERIVATION).
- **Application:** 61,440 × 256 × 64 × 3 ≈ 3.0e9 MAC → a few ms (ESTIMATE; gather / memory bound).
- **K_far:** a coarse modal or coarse-solve term, about 1 ms (ESTIMATE).
- **Rebuild:** rigid 0. Dent: interpolator evaluation over about 1e3–2e3 ports × 16 bins × 256 receivers × 64 bins ≈ 3e8–5e8 outputs, about 0.1–0.5 s (ESTIMATE).
- **Parameters:** tables ≈ 4e7 values; dent net about 1e5–1e6.

### Potential failure cases
- **The equivalence cutoff fails for red far field or coarse metrics.** It was measured only on the near field and fine16 contrast at one pair, and L_R ≈ 18.3 mm ≈ the cutoff. The class count would then grow (for example a cap of 15 → about 136 top classes) and storage with it.
- Path-splitting composition may diverge, or be inaccurate, if the re-crossing norm is near 1 for red.
- Window truncation for red interior sources (5.5 % beyond 128 ports) depends on the quality of K_far.
- The table approach is exact only for this rest box. For general shapes every port is its own context, and CCA degenerates into C1/C2 plus an interpolator.
- The dent interpolator reintroduces the coverage and extrapolation risk, now in shape space.
- MC table noise (32M photons per packet) propagates directly into outputs, and is reduced only by averaging replicas.

### Closest prior work
(Specialists' identities, partly from memory.)
- Moon, Walter & Marschner 2007: precomputed canonical shell transport reused across a medium.
- Loos et al. 2011, Modular Radiance Transfer: precomputed reusable modules with interfaces.
- Response-matrix / interface-current methods (Lindahl & Weiss 1981) and COMET (Mosher & Rahnema 2006).
- Invariant embedding / first-crossing decompositions in transport theory.
- Group-equivariant CNNs (Cohen & Welling 2016) and learned canonicalisation (Kaba et al. 2023), for the gauge-fixing view.

### Plausible novel contribution (INFERENCE)
- Turning a *measured* context-equivalence result into an exact indexing rule for a reusable intrinsic operator.
- Composing never-supervised corner classes from edge kernels by an exact path-splitting identity, which is uncommon in graphics.
- A steerable-to-bin projection to keep dented poses gauge-consistent with a square-to-disk bin contract.

### Minimal falsification experiment
1. **Equivalence-class test (≤ 8 new packets, existing pipeline).** Choose 2–3 source pairs that the class map predicts equivalent by translation, not by symmetry, including one corner-adjacent and one side-rim pair. Compare them on native near field, fine16 contrast, **abs_coarse and red far-field tallies**. Falsified if any pair differs by > 3× noise. The atlas premise then fails, or needs a larger cutoff with re-costing.
2. **Composition test (existing references).** Predict one existing BND_FAR corner / vertical-edge reference from edge-class kernels via path splitting. Falsified if the result exceeds the gates, or exceeds 1.25× the error of that source's own per-source oracle.
3. **Rest coverage test.** Build all tables by MC, then score the whole independent INT / BND_NEAR / BND_FAR population. Falsified if any stratum misses the gates, given step 1 passed. That would be a window or K_far failure, not a context failure.
4. **Dent** (only if steps 1–3 pass): the interpolator signed-change gates on a held-out dent pose.

## Candidate C4: NSM: collision-order-split port-space operator — exact low orders (near field) + albedo-generic slow-mode tail of the geometric flight kernel

**Central mechanism.** Precompute K in port space from the exact factorisation, so that runtime application is ms-scale without an inner solve. The split is by collision order. Orders 0..N, which spread only about √(2N) mfp, are computed exactly per channel and stored as a sparse near-field operator. Orders above N are represented exactly by the slow eigenmodes of the channel-free, volume-self-adjoint geometric kernel W, projected through the exact flights. Channels, and any albedo edit, enter the tail only through the scalar spectral filter ω^{N+2}λ^{N+1}/(1 − ωλ). The modes are kept as whole D4h-degenerate eigenspaces, which keeps exact equivariance. Compared with C1, this trades a slower geometry rebuild (tens of seconds) for a faster application (a few ms). Sources: physics B (spectral resolvent), neural A (albedo/collision-order series T_n, matrix peeling), critic (H-compressed precompute). The order-split construction and the equivariant-truncation rule are my own additions.

### Physical operator
W is self-adjoint in the cell-volume inner product M (V_x W[x,y] = V_y W[y,x]). Write W = V Λ Vᵀ M, with V M-orthonormal.

`K_c = Σ_{n=0}^{N} ω_c^{n+1} T_n  +  Ψout · diag( ω_c^{N+2} λ_k^{N+1} / (1 − ω_c λ_k) ) · Ψinᵀ`

- `T_n = Φout W^n F_in`
- `Ψout = Φout V_k`, of size (port·bin) × k
- `Ψin = F_inᵀ M V_k`

V_k holds the k slowest modes (λ ≥ λ_cut), truncated only at eigenvalue gaps between whole degenerate eigenspaces.

**Tail truncation bound (DERIVATION):** discarded modes contribute at most ω^{N+2} λ_cut^{N+1}/(1 − ωλ_cut) per unit projected weight.
- Example: N = 40 and λ_cut ≈ 0.84 give ≈ 0.84^{41}/0.16 ≈ 5e-3 (ESTIMATE).
- Transport eigenvalue estimate λ(k) ≈ arctan(k)/k (in mfp⁻¹) with slab vertical modes. λ ≥ 0.84 corresponds to k ≲ 0.75 mm⁻¹, which suggests about 1e3 modes: about 24 lateral harmonics per axis × 2–3 vertical modes (SPECULATION on the exact count; the specialists estimated 1e2–1e3).

### Explicit information
- F_in, Φout and W (as in C1).
- The near-field per-channel matrix Σ_{n≤N} ω^{n+1}T_n, computed by C1-style batched solves.
- V_k, from Lanczos / subspace iteration on W.

### Learned information
- None required. The representation is a computed compression.
- Optional: a learned predictor of the mode update under a dent, as a warm start. It affects speed only.

### Source conditioning
Through exact F_in, in the precomputed columns of the near field and in Ψin. No learned function of the source.

### Geometry conditioning
Geometry is baked into the near-field columns and the modes, and rebuilt after a geometry change.

### Angular and spatial representation
- **Native port × bin** in the near field. Sparse window: about 144 receivers (12×12 ports, ±15 mm) plus the opposite face.
- **Volume modes** projected to port × bin through the exact flights.
- No shared angular basis.

### Transformation behaviour
- **D4h:** exact if the near field is built with symmetric quadrature and the truncation keeps whole degenerate eigenspaces. Irrep projectors commute with the group (my DERIVATION). The near field can also be stored once per symmetry orbit (8–16× less storage).
- **Rigid motion:** invariant.
- **Dent:**
  - Recompute the near-field columns of the sources in the dent influence region: about 1e3 ports × 16 bins. Batch them with multiplexed sources separated by more than the window (matrix probing / peeling, Lin, Lu & Ying 2011).
  - Update the modes by warm-started subspace iteration (a few hundred W applications).
- **Material (albedo) edit:** the tail re-filters instantly and exactly. The near field needs recomputation, unless per-order or Chebyshev-in-ω blocks are stored (HYPOTHESIS on affordability).

### Information preservation
The split is exact. The losses are:
- (i) window truncation of low orders: random-walk rms 3-D displacement after n flights is √(2n) mm, about 9 mm at N = 40, so mass beyond ±15 mm is estimated at about 1–3 % in red (ESTIMATE; must be measured, and can be routed to a coarse correction);
- (ii) discarded fast modes (≈ 5e-3);
- (iii) the C1 discretisation underneath.

### Physical invariants and how they are guaranteed
- **Linearity, zero-in / zero-out:** structural.
- **Non-negativity:** the near field is ≥ 0 (exact orders). The tail is **not** guaranteed: only the Perron mode is positive, and the other modes are signed.
- **Passivity:** not structural. Check it per column against the exact budget from a C1 adjoint (w*). Enforce it with a geometry-only column scale, which keeps linearity. The HYPOTHESIS is that negative tail mass stays below MC noise for N ≥ 40.
- **Equivariance:** exact (above).
- **Reciprocity:** the tail is symmetric if Φout and F_in are reverse-ray consistent; the near field is as in C1.
- **Ownership and ballistic complementarity:** inherited from C1.

### Generalisation mechanism
- Identical to C1. Every column of every source is computed, so there is no unseen source.
- This is a runtime representation of the solved physics, not a separate learning claim. Its validity is conditional on C1 passing.

### Training supervision
- None. MC for validation only.
- The C1 solver generates the operator.
- Optional (neural A): a single MC run with collision-order tallies validates T_n for all channels at once.

### Computational complexity (ESTIMATES)
- **Near field:** 61,440 source bins × ~150 receivers × 64 bins × 3 channels ≈ 1.8e9 entries → about 3.5 GB in fp16. About 0.2–0.5 GB if stored per symmetry orbit plus interior translation sharing.
- **Tail:** Ψout 245,760 × 1e3 ≈ 2.5e8 (1 GB fp32); Ψin 61,440 × 1e3 ≈ 6e7 (0.25 GB). Per-channel tail application ≈ 3e8 MAC.
- **Application:** about 2–5 ms for RGB.
- **Precompute per geometry:**
  - Near field: about 1e4 batched multiplexed solves × 40 W applications × ~1–3 ms ≈ 10–30 min.
  - Modes: Lanczos with about 2–3e3 W applications ≈ 1–5 s.
- **Dent rebuild:** about 1e3 ports × 16 bins multiplexed into a few hundred batched solves → about 10–60 s, plus a mode update of about 1 s.
- **Comparison:** rebuild is slower than C1 (≤ 1 s) and slower than path-traced re-rendering of a small workload (a few seconds). Application is faster than C1 (5–40 ms).
- NSM pays off only when there are many lighting / visibility changes per geometry change.

### Potential failure cases
- **The mode count is larger than estimated.** The slab's lateral extent gives a dense slow spectrum, and boundary-layer modes may matter. Storage then exceeds 12 GB.
- **Signed modal tail** produces negative entries or passivity violations above noise.
- **Window truncation of red low orders** is larger than estimated.
- **Rebuild cost** makes NSM unsuitable for frequently deforming geometry. This is a structural trade-off, not a bug.
- **Inherits every C1 discretisation risk** (edges and corners).
- **Near-degenerate eigenvalues** under a dent make subspace tracking unstable.

### Closest prior work
(Specialists' identities, partly from memory.)
- Precomputed radiance transfer and its compression: Sloan et al. 2002/2003 (clustered PCA, including subsurface); Wang, Tran & Luebke 2005 (all-frequency translucent relighting).
- Hierarchical-matrix construction from matrix–vector products (Lin, Lu & Ying 2011).
- Learning Green's functions from few forcings with randomized linear algebra (Boullé & Townsend, FoCM ~2023).
- Modal / eigenfunction expansions of the transport operator in reactor physics.
- Hašan et al. 2006, direct-to-indirect transfer.

### Plausible novel contribution (INFERENCE)
- A collision-order split, rather than a spatial near/far split, so that the stored tail is both exact in form and **albedo-generic** (channels and material edits differ by a scalar spectral filter of one geometric eigenbasis).
- An equivariant-by-construction truncation rule.
- Used as a ms-scale reusable intrinsic K with exact flights.

### Minimal falsification experiment
This is a runtime-representation test. It needs no MC, and it presupposes C1 at the same discretisation.

1. Build NSM at rest for N ∈ {20, 40} and λ_cut ∈ {0.8, 0.84, 0.9}. Compare it column by column against converged C1 on the S16-R sources and on the INT / BND populations.
2. **Measure:** relative error per stratum against C1, negative-mass fraction per column, Σ ≤ d violations, exact symmetry equality, memory and application time.
3. **Falsified as a viable runtime form if any of the following holds:**
   - reaching ≤ 1/3 of each gate margin relative to C1 needs > 8 GB;
   - negative tail mass > 1 % of any column;
   - application is not faster than C1;
   - dent rebuild > 60 s.
4. **Physics validity** is delegated to C1's falsification. Where C1 is falsified, NSM is moot.
