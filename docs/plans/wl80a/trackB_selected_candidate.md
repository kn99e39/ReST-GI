## Rejected mechanisms

## Rejected mechanisms and the decisive reason for each

### Rejected whole candidates
- **C4 NSM: rejected as a candidate.** The decisive reasons are these.
  1. It is a stored table of every response column computed by C1. It has no generalisation or learnability mechanism of its own (the developer conceded R2.1 as fatal).
  2. Its spectrally truncated tail is signed, so it violates the non-negativity contract by construction. No repair keeps both the modal tail and non-negativity (conceded R1.3).
  3. A dent rebuild costs minutes against a few seconds to re-render (conceded R2.2).
  
  A residual use survives only as an *analysis instrument*: collision-order decomposition of C1 or MC tallies. It is not a deployable K.
- **C3 CCA: rejected as an answer to the central question.** At rest it is enumeration by construction, and the developer accepts REJECT for the full candidate. Its narrowed "CCA-rest" form is kept only as a non-learned **diagnostic control** of the local-context premise. It is the only proposed check that does not depend on the Peierls discretisation. It is not selected as a candidate because it makes no generalisation claim.
- **C2 SBG: not selected (deferred variant of C1, not a separate survivor).** The developer's own amended verdict says the learned component is MARGINAL. On the box every chart kernel can be computed exactly by reduced solves (D3), so learning there is compression, not information. The dent compression (H-matrix rank) is unproven. Its passivity route (D2) and its pipeline check (step 0) need a validated C1-type discrete W. The mechanism is therefore not substantially different from C1: the same flights, the same scalar resolvent, the same S1, with only a different representation of the resolvent. Its case is not independently defensible until C1 passes. It is preserved as a conditional follow-on (see verdict, unresolved points).

### Rejected sub-mechanisms inside surviving or deferred designs
- **C1, original split form** (exact point S1 + flat-cell continuation). It breaches passivity. I re-derived the counterexample: continuation 0.523 / 0.620 of q at μ = 0.01 / 0.1, against 0.722 assigned to both, so ΣK reaches about 1.08–1.12·d for red at near-horizon bins. Replaced by a consistent point-level first continuation W1.
- **C1, per-cell partition of unity "by construction" with independent kernel and reverse-beam quadratures.** A per-cell residual of about 1e-3 already threatens red passivity. Replaced by complement-defined escape, with a reported reciprocity defect.
- **C1, uniform lattice with staircase dent re-mask and a learned row-sum-preserving ΔW closure.** ΔW cannot change the escape fraction, which is the first-order cut-cell error. Rest has no cut cells and the dent does, so the biases do not cancel in the signed change. Replaced by a body-fitted graded mesh; ΔW dropped.
- **C1, uniform h = 0.25 mm route.** Does not fit 12 GB and refines in the wrong place.
- **C1, "one W application per outer GI iteration" cost claim.** It inherits ρ ≈ 0.974 for red, about 260 outer iterations. Withdrawn.
- **C1, falsifier "fail only if above gate *and* plateaued".** It cannot reject a slowly converging first-order error. Replaced by fail at the finest affordable level within a predeclared budget.
- **C1, truncated Krylov as "exactly linear".** Withdrawn; only a converged solve or a fixed linear iteration qualifies.
- **C2, Ĝ = vis·G∞·τ.** Visibility on the resolvent deletes real multiply-scattered transport around the dent cavity. The flight factorisation (D1) puts visibility inside each W factor.
- **C2, product composition of single-wall factors for unsupervised corners.** It is biased low already in the steady-state Dirichlet wedge: images are additive, and r1²r4² − r2²r3² < 0. No composition rule is known in the transport boundary layer.
- **C2, G∞ as the [0,1]-bounded base in red.** The bound is near-vacuous: ∫G∞ = 1/(1−ω) = 1000 against about 38 slab collisions.
- **C2 option (c), fine-tuning on S16-R packets.** It reintroduces the measured late-training transfer degradation (§3b).
- **C3, corner path-splitting composition.** It needs interior-plane crossing tallies and interior-plane sources that no reference contains, it is ill-posed from boundary data, its Toeplitz/FFT claim is false for corners, and it loses sign and passivity guarantees.
- **C3, steerable dent interpolator.** It has a hidden side-face-distance variable, incoming-bin non-identifiability under rotation, a C1/C2-circular supervision source, and it is the same geometry-feature→response family whose failure is recorded in §3b.
- **C3 and C4, reciprocity symmetrisation under the 16-in / 64-out bin contract.** Ill-posed, because the reverse of an outgoing bin is not an incoming bin.

### Cross-cutting mechanism the panel converged on rejecting
**Any representation in which source identity or source context passes through a learned function**, including geometry-relative features, consistency penalties and shared angular profiles. This rejection is an INFERENCE, not a measured causal attribution. The basis is:
- the family-level OUTCOME in §3b;
- the derivation that, for this medium, all incoming-direction dependence enters through an exactly computable F_in, so a learned source map has nothing to supply that physics does not already give exactly.

The brief does not measure the causal shares of data volume, invariance, optimisation and angular basis in the measured failures, and the panel did not determine them.

## Selected candidate specification

## Selected primary: C1 CP-K (amended), an explicit collision-probability intrinsic operator

### 1. Physical and mathematical assumptions
- **FACT (brief):**
  - homogeneous medium;
  - isotropic phase function;
  - σ_t = 1000 m⁻¹ in all channels;
  - albedo 0.999 / 0.99 / 0.9;
  - index-matched boundary: a photon that reaches the boundary leaves;
  - ballistic part handled separately by B, with d = 1 − β.
- **DERIVATION (Peierls / collision-probability form).** Under these assumptions the post-collision state is the scalar collision density. Angle enters only through the first flight (F_in) and the last flight (Φ). Channels differ only through the scalar ω_c.
- **Scope (conceded).** Every claim below is limited to isotropic, homogeneous, index-matched media. An anisotropic phase function (angular moments inside the medium), Fresnel/TIR (reflected chords, loss of the escape-only structure) or heterogeneity (no FFT) each changes the state and the cost.

### 2. Operator (amended form, per channel c)
```
q   = F_in u                         (first-collision density at fine points p; Σ_p q = d(i,a) by shared rays with B)
ψ   = ω W1 q + ω W ψ                 (cell collision density from the second collision on)
K u = ω Φ_pt q + ω Φout ψ
```
- **F_in.** An exact ray integral of the incident port × bin packet under the contract weighting. It uses the same rays as B, so complementarity holds to float precision.
- **Φ_pt[·; p] and W1[·; p].** One shared stratified isotropic ray set per fine first-collision point gives uncollided escape to (port, bin) and next-collision deposit into cells. The partition 1ᵀΦ_pt[p] + 1ᵀW1[p] = 1 is exact by telescoping. A chord that leaves the medium, including into the dent cavity, terminates; re-entry belongs to C.
- **W.** The cell-to-cell flight kernel, built by ray telescoping so that 1ᵀW[y] ≤ 1.
- **Φout.** Its per-cell total is *defined* as the complement 1 − 1ᵀW[y]. Quadrature is used only for the shape over (j, o), with the reference's radiance-weighted convention.
- **Energy identity (DERIVATION, re-verified by the chair):** total output = ω·1ᵀq − (1−ω)·1ᵀψ ≤ ω·d < d. It holds at convergence for any h and any W accuracy. A truncated Neumann iteration only underestimates.

### 3. Explicit versus learned information
- **Explicit:** the mesh, the port frames, in-medium visibility, F_in, W1, Φ_pt, W, Φout, ω_c, σ_t, B and d.
- **Learned:** none. An optional learned preconditioner could affect speed only.
- **Source conditioning:** only the right-hand side F_in u. No learned function ever sees a source.
- **Geometry conditioning:** the body-fitted mesh and the ray integrals on the true surface.

### 4. Discretisation
- **Mesh:** body-fitted, anisotropically graded, about 2–3e5 cells (ESTIMATE).
  - Lateral spacing about 1.25 mm.
  - Depth layers 0.05, 0.05, 0.1, 0.1, 0.2, 0.2, 0.4 mm, … toward the mid-plane, on all faces.
  - Edges graded as a product in the cross-section and translation-shared along the edge.
- **Rest pose:** per-face translation invariance lets F_in, Φout and the point templates be applied as convolutions, with little memory.
- **W application:** explicit near field within about 3 mfp (about 1e8 non-zeros, about 0.7 GB), plus a precorrected-FFT far field. The developer's suggestion for an FFT that pushes 1ᵀW above 1 is clamp-and-renormalise. Any such adjustment must preserve the complement definition and be reported.
- **Dented pose:** the same mesh topology deformed by a vertical stretch map, so there are no cut cells. Flights near the dent are ray-integrated on the true mesh.
- **Equivariance:** matrices assembled from orbit representatives by permutation give exact D4h equivariance. Otherwise equivariance holds to about 1e-7.
- **Solver:** converged to 1e-6 relative, or a fixed linear iteration. A coarse correction is accepted only with non-negative R/P and a sub-stochastic coarse operator. Any overshoot counts as a passivity breach.

### 5. Transformation behaviour
- **Rigid motion:** invariant in the body frame; nothing to rebuild.
- **D4h:** exact permutation equivariance at rest, as above.
- **Dent:** recompute the templates and flights for ports within the influence support. That support must include bottom, side and rim sources whose transport changes (D_T ≠ D_G), not only top ports. Re-solve.
- **Signed change:** the same topology and resolution at rest and dented makes the discretisation biases correlated (INFERENCE). Whether they cancel is a HYPOTHESIS.

### 6. Complexity (ESTIMATES only; nothing measured)
| Item | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Per K application | 10–40 W applications × 5–20 ms → about 0.05–0.8 s per RGB application for a dense incident field |
| GI solve, 10–30 outer iterations with warm-started inner solves | about 1–10 s |
| Dent template rebuild | about 1–5 s on the fly |
| Cached tabulated alternative | about 10 ms per application, but minutes per dent |
| Memory | about 1–3 GB plus solver state; fits 12 GB at the graded resolution |

Against the brief's comparators (ms-scale learned application, a few seconds of path-traced re-rendering), C1 is **slower per application than the learned operators, and probably not cheaper than re-rendering under deformation** (INFERENCE). Lighting-only reuse at fixed geometry may be favourable; that is unmeasured. A coupled K+C diffusion-synthetic acceleration is undesigned.

### 7. Learnability assessment
There is nothing to learn, so there is no memorisation, training drift or sparse-supervision failure mode (DERIVATION). The 4–8 MC replicas serve only as an independent validator. Consequence: C1 answers brief Q4 with "for this medium the intrinsic K has no learnable content that physics does not already supply exactly". The developer and both reviewers concede this is a substitution, not an answer to whether a *learned* K can generalise.

### 8. Novelty assessment (INFERENCE)
Low. The numerical ingredients are classical:
- collision-probability / Peierls methods, first-collision source, DSA;
- response-matrix / interface-current methods, with COMET as the structural analogue of the K/C split;
- graphics precedents: zonal method, shell transport functions (Moon et al. 2007; Müller et al. 2016), Modular Radiance Transfer.

What remains is system integration under this specific contract:
- the port/bin first-exit contract;
- exact ballistic complementarity;
- a passivity identity exact at every h under the consistent split;
- exact D4h permutation equivariance;
- body-fitted local rebuild.

The research-relevant reframing is the most valuable contribution: *unseen-source generalisation becomes a discretisation-convergence question that can be checked without references.*

### 9. Strongest counterexamples (unresolved)
1. **Two- and three-face boundary layers at corners, vertical edges and side rims.** These are exactly the BND_FAR strata.
   - The order-1 escape and the order-2 collision distribution are point-exact in the amended form.
   - The order-2 escape onward is cell-averaged and converges only first order while h > μ_out. At grazing outgoing bins (μ_out ≈ 0.05) the bias is still about 2 % at 0.1 mfp layers (DERIVATION, developer).
   - Whether con_coarse ≤ 0.25 holds on BND_FAR within budget is the central HYPOTHESIS.
2. **Dent signed change.** A differential bias between rest and dent in the thinned 4 mm region, where the slab-mode lateral scale falls below the port size. It threatens the 0.35 / 0.90 gates.
3. **Convention mismatch.** The port/bin radiance-weighting convention or the "boundary-near 1.8–4 mm" source definition may not match the reference. Port centres lie at 1.25 + 2.5k mm, and 1.8 mm matches none; this is unresolved from the brief.
4. **Cost.** By estimate, under deformation a solve plus rebuild is about 1–10 s against "a few seconds" of re-rendering. The brief's value criterion could be failed even if the physics passes.
5. **Reciprocity defect.** Exact partition, exact volume-weighted reciprocity and a non-negative complement cannot all hold at finite quadrature. The developer chose partition and passivity.

### 10. Minimal falsification experiment (predeclared before any run; no training)
0. **Convention gate.**
   - (a) Half-space H-function albedo at normal and grazing incidence, ≤ 1 %.
   - (b) Single-scatter Φ_pt·q against an MC single-scatter tally. This needs a short new MC run if the packets carry no collision orders.
   
   A miss stops interpretation.
1. **Three graded levels.** Each level halves all graded sizes. Budget ≤ 6 GPU-h, peak ≤ 11 GB.
2. **Evaluation.** Score rest and the existing dented pose on:
   - the S16-R held-out replicas 4–7;
   - the never-used INT / BND_NEAR / BND_FAR populations;
   - the brief's metrics as worst-channel upper-95 % bounds.
3. **Falsified if any of the following holds.**
   - (a) Any stratum misses abs_coarse 0.10, abs_fine16 0.15 or con_coarse 0.25 at the finest affordable level. A still-decreasing error is no excuse.
   - (b) Any column has ΣK > d·(1 + 1e-6).
   - (c) The dent signed change has relative error > 0.35 or correlation < 0.90 in any channel.
   - (d) BND_FAR exceeds max(1.25 × INT, INT + 0.03) on any metric.
   
   Observed order and Richardson estimates are diagnostics only. A gate miss with observed order < 0.5 between the two finest levels is classified as a model, contract or reference mismatch, and it is still a fail.
4. **Paired contrast control (chair's clarification).** The bin-averaged-F_in run gives con → 0 *by construction*. Alone it is uninformative. It is evidential only when paired with the exact-F_in con_* against the reference: the exact-F_in operator must reproduce the reference contrast, and the averaged one must collapse it.
5. **Cost recorded separately.** Cost does not falsify the physics, but it decides the deployability verdict: application time, GI solve time and dent rebuild, against the path-traced comparator at matched quality.
6. **Optional independent control (chair's suggestion; not part of C1).** Run C3-rest test 1 (context-locality pairs on native, fine16, abs_coarse, con_coarse, far tail and side exits). Its premise is MC-only. If C1 fails while converging, this control helps separate a discretisation failure from a contract or reference issue.

## Final Track B verdict

## Final Track B verdict (synthesis chair)

**Selection.**
- **Primary: C1, CP-K in its amended form.**
- **Secondary: NONE.**
- C2 is deferred as a conditional variant of C1, not as an independent survivor.
- C3 and C4 are rejected as candidates. CCA-rest is kept as a non-learned diagnostic control of the local-context premise. NSM's order decomposition is kept only as an analysis instrument.

**Does any surviving design give a credible explanation of generalisation to unseen sources?**

*Yes, mechanistically, for this test medium only.*
- Under isotropic, homogeneous, index-matched transport, every incoming-direction dependence enters through an exact, computable first-flight integral (DERIVATION).
- C1 contains no source-indexed or context-indexed fitted quantity. Its error is therefore independent of which sources were supervised.
- The question "will it transfer to unsupervised BND_FAR contexts?" becomes "does the boundary-layer discretisation converge to the gates within budget?". That can be tested without training, and it is a HYPOTHESIS.

This explanation is consistent with the brief's family-level OUTCOME, but it does **not** establish a cause for that outcome.
- The panel infers that passing source identity or context through a learned function forces extrapolation in context space.
- The causal shares of data volume, invariance, optimisation and angular basis in the measured failures are unmeasured.

**Confidence (based on argument strength, not agent count).**

| Claim | Confidence | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Factorisation and source-agnostic mechanism | High | Exact algebra under the stated FACTs. Re-derived independently by five specialists and by me. The amended passivity identity checks. The original split-form breach was confirmed numerically by hand. |
| Physical invariants of the amended form | High at convergence | Linearity, non-negativity, passivity and D4h equivariance. Solver overshoot and the reciprocity defect must be measured. |
| Meeting the gates on BND_FAR corners, vertical edges and side rims at an affordable graded resolution | Unknown; moderate plausibility | Point-exact low orders and near-field dominance argue for it. First-order cell error in two- and three-face boundary layers at grazing bins argues against. |
| Dent signed-change gates | Low to moderate | Correlated-bias cancellation is an INFERENCE only. |
| Deployability under the brief's value criterion (cheaper than re-rendering while accurate) | Low | ESTIMATED 1–10 s per GI solve plus seconds of dent rebuild, against a few seconds of re-render. Lighting-only reuse at fixed geometry is the one plausible favourable regime, and it is unmeasured. |
| Novelty | Low | System integration of classical transport numerics. |

**What the selection does not deliver.**
- It does not answer the brief's *learned*-K question. For this medium, the panel's answer to Q2 is "everything can be explicit; nothing needs to be learned". Whether that is acceptable is a research-owner decision.
- It does not transfer to anisotropic phase functions, Fresnel boundaries or heterogeneous media without a different interior state.
- It does not demonstrate a cost advantage.

**Unresolved disagreements, preserved.**
1. **Learned versus explicit.** The reviewers hold that C1 "replaces learning rather than answering it"; the developer concedes this. Whether the project's central question permits a non-learned K remains open.
2. **The value of C2's chart-space learning.** The C2 developer argues that learning geometry charts rather than sources is the only coherent role left for learning. The reviewers argue it adds compression, not information, on a box, and that its dent compression is unproven. This is unresolved until C1 is validated and a cost comparison exists.
3. **Discretisation route.** Uniform FFT lattice versus graded body-fitted mesh. The amended C1 commits to graded, but the precorrected-FFT far-field consistency with complement-defined escape is unverified.
4. **Sign of the flat-Φout outgoing bias.** Reviewer: negative at grazing. Developer: about +4.6 % at μ = 0.4 and h = 0.5 mfp. The magnitude is agreed at a few percent; the sign is unresolved.
5. **Red context-equivalence cutoff.** The infinite-medium length of about 18 mm (candidate) versus the leakage-dominated slab-mode length of about 3.6 mm (reviewers; consistent with the measured 5.5 % tail). The chair finds the reviewers' derivation more applicable. It still must be measured, because diffusion is poor within a few mfp of the boundary.
6. **Contract ambiguities in the brief.** The "1.8–4 mm" boundary-near source distance matches no port-centre distance. Whether the MC packets carry collision-order tallies is not stated, and it decides whether step 0(b) needs new MC.
7. **Cost.** Specialist and developer estimates range over more than an order of magnitude (5–150 ms per W-level application; 1–10 s per GI solve). No candidate's cost is measured.

**Authority note.** This is a thought-experiment selection. It authorises no implementation, training, measurement or successor batch.
