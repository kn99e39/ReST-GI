## Revised specification (refinement lead: mathematics, information flow, complexity)

# Outcome: BGC-Transport is REJECTED (Track A refinement lead, 2026-11-09)

This is a thought experiment. No files were written, nothing was run, and there are no new measurements. I re-checked these repository facts this session:
- WL75 lines 195, 207 and 308;
- WL78 lines 119, 246 and 275;
- WL76 lines 102-110, 207-208 and 270.

No revised architecture is adopted. BGC's central hypothesis is that pair-conditioned angular-spatial factors, generated from first-flight and relational encoders under an energy normalisation, give held-out source transfer. That hypothesis is not supported:
- **No identification.** Its hard constraints (linear, positive, passive, rest-D4h-equivariant, identity-free) leave out-of-orbit boundary contexts unidentified. This follows from the orbit-consistent bump construction (DERIVATION).
- **Wrong target.** Its only new element (A3) acts on the factor the record ranks as non-binding (FACT, WL75 l.308).
- **Already tested.** Its other parts re-assemble mechanisms that were already tested: FIA, WL76 INSUFFICIENT FIT; W+UV-Q, WL78 INCONCLUSIVE; rest D4h maps, WL79/80.

The corrections below fix defects in validity. None of them adds identification.

## 1. Element-level corrections (record only; not an architecture)

These are the corrections justified by specific audit defects. They are recorded so the defects are not repeated. Assembling them does not give an adopted model.

**(C1) Budget, from A1/A4.**
- d(i,a) = 1 − β(i,a), with β(i,a) = (1/m(i,a)) ∫_port ∫_bin exp(−σ_t·t(x,ω)) cosθ dω dA.
- Computed on a fixed deterministic grid of analytic chords on the current mesh. No Monte Carlo.
- m(i,a) = A_i × the projected solid angle of bin a.
- Collision probability is dropped as a feature because it equals d.

**(C2) Passive operator, from A4 and the normalisation.**
- K(i,a→j,o,c) = d(i,a)·ω_c·χ_D(i,j)·m(j,o)·z(i,a,j,o,c) / max(1, S(i,a,c))
- S(i,a,c) = Σ_{j',o'} χ_D(i,j')·m(j',o')·z(i,a,j',o',c), with z ≥ 0.
- χ_D is the exact same-optical-domain mask, which keeps FNTO K/C ownership.
- z = z_loc on N(i) and z_far on its complement, with disjoint supports and one shared denominator.
- ρ and ε are removed.
- Properties (DERIVATION):
  - linear in the incident field, provided z depends only on geometry and is never computed from the actual illumination;
  - Σ_{j,o} K ≤ ω_c·d, so ||K||_1 ≤ max ω_c·d < 1;
  - entrywise local while S ≤ 1;
  - reciprocal while S ≤ 1, if z is reciprocal.
- The max(1,·) projection must be predeclared as part of the contract and the fraction of columns where it binds must be reported. It must never be added after a failure.

**(C3) Factor gauge, from the Z operator form.** If z is factorised, merge w into u and normalise v per r under m(j,o). The remaining freedom is a permutation over r only.

**(C4) Relational inputs, from A2.**
- Boundary distances clipped at r_c ≈ 5/κ_c ≈ 18 / 16 / 8 mm for R/G/B. This uses the DERIVATION decay lengths 3.5 / 3.1 / 1.6 mm (diffusion lowest slab mode, H_eff ≈ 11.3 mm).
- A corridor descriptor over a band of width about sqrt(r·ℓ) around the i-j segment.
- Neighbour sets defined by radius with all ties included.
- One frame convention defined from geometry rather than triangle index.
- No rest or material coordinates.
- A declared σ_t law under deformation.

**(C5) Symmetry, from A5.**
- Exact rest-D4h maps (WL79) used only for training-time augmentation and as a predeclared test.
- If inference averaging is used, average the assembled K, including d and S, and store it dense.
- No claim for deformed poses, continuous SO(2) or the per-port frame gauge.

**(C6) Rank and representation, from the rank form and A6.** UNRESOLVED. Per-pair nonnegative rank at the target accuracy, by pair class (near < 5 mm, edge/corner, far) against a replica noise floor, has never been measured. The local/far support needs a smooth partition window, latents produced only by an encoder, and a fused receiver-major apply.

## 2. Repairs that change the central hypothesis (reported, NOT adopted, NOT recommended)

- **(R-a) Exact first-collision anchoring.** K = d·E_{p1}[Q(x→j,o)] with a learned source-independent Q (WL76 E0, FACT). This is the FIA family. Its recorded limit was the ring-lobe angular floor (0.45 residual, WL76 l.270).
- **(R-b) Reciprocity-symmetric bilinear form.** m_i K_ij = (ω/4πσ_t)·(m d)_{i,a}·(m d)_{j,ō}·<p1_i, 𝒢 p1_j>, with a learned symmetric 𝒢 (DERIVATION, E1). This is a learned symmetric Green's-kernel architecture.
- **(R-c) Shared atoms with pair coefficients.** This is a higher-rank global ATO (WL68/74/75 family).

None of these is BGC. Each would need its own user decision. This review recommends none of them.

## 3. Information-flow specification of BGC as proposed (shows the missing identification)

- **Geometry G_t:**
  - → A1 source-side (d, μ, Σ) per (i,a). Receiver-side p1 is absent.
  - → A2 endpoint contexts, relative displacement, frames and unclipped distances. No corridor. Leakage routes: ties, distances, frames.
  - → shared generator f_θ → (w, u, v) per (i,j,r,c), and ρ per (i,a,c)
  - → Z → K = dρZ/(ε+ΣZ)
  - → A5 averaging over 16 group images (rest only)
  - → C coupling → solve.
- **Supervision:** 16 one-hot sources (S16-R: 12 interior, which collapse to about one relative context; 4 boundary orbits at 1.8-4.0 mm, FACT WL73/79), with dense receivers.
- **What reaches out-of-orbit boundary or deformed contexts:**
  - only f_θ's inductive bias;
  - A4 and A5 constrain nothing there (bump construction);
  - reciprocity, the only exact cross-column constraint, is excluded by the independent ρ.

## Novelty assessment

## Novelty assessment

**Verdict: NOT SUPPORTED as a new mechanism (INFERENCE).** Every element A1–A6 maps exactly onto prior art, project history, or both. Any contribution would be empirical only and would need a measured result, per RESEARCH_CENTRIC_TOPIC.md and FNTO contract §8.

### Element mapping

**A3 + A4 operator (mass × normalised conditional exit distribution, written as a mixture of separable lobes)**
- 8DNA uses F = α·p(·|·), per asset (V-WL77A, not re-checked).
- Vicini–Koltun–Jakob 2019 is a learned shape-adaptive SSS model: absorption plus a conditional exit sampler, with generalisation to unseen shapes (verified by the novelty specialist this session).
- Neural Parametric Mixtures, SIGGRAPH 2023 (verified this session).
- Mixture density networks (Bishop 1994; unverified).
- Conditional low rank is the DeepONet / hypernetwork low-rank head. HyperDeepONet arXiv 2507.18346 was checked at snippet level only.
- **Within the project:** global ATO K = d Σ_r A_r T_ijr O_r (WL68/74/75, FACT). BGC is its hypernetwork generalisation with u and v made pair-dependent (DERIVATION).

**A1 incident encoder**
- The first-collision source method, Jensen 2001 and Photon Beam Diffusion (V-WL77A).
- DeepRTE arXiv 2505.23190: an optical-depth encoder plus a learned Green's function trained from delta inflows. It has no positivity or energy constraint and needs retraining for a new geometry (verified this session).
- **Within the project:** the FIA E0 factorisation (WL76, FACT). Collision probability = d = 1−β (FACT).

**A2 relation encoder**
- **Within the project:** essentially W+UV-Q (WL78: INCONCLUSIVE; BND_FAR 0.668 / 0.860; worse on deformation) and RSK (WL72: no gain) (FACT).
- Prior art: Vicini 2019 local polynomial, Deep Scattering 2017, EGNN-style relative features (V-WL77A).

**A5 frame transformation**
- Frame averaging (Puny et al., ICLR 2022, arXiv 2110.03336; verified this session). Over the finite D4h group it reduces to the Reynolds average.
- **Within the project:** WL79 Stage 0 exact maps; WL80 orbit-balanced basis (FACT).

**A6 computational representation**
- **Within the project:** the W (128-NN) + UV tail split; WL76's 512-NN softmax plus 240-cell tail (FACT).
- Prior art: H-matrices, multipole GNO, hierarchical radiosity, PRT/CPCA cached coefficients (V-WL77A).

**Closest structural precedent**
- Element Learning (Du & Stechmann, arXiv 2308.02467; verified this session). It is a geometry-conditioned learned boundary-to-boundary RTE operator composed by an HDG solver (INFERENCE).

**Adjacent, not shared**
- Neural radiance caching (Müller 2021) is a nonlinear online cache, not an operator.
- TransGI shares only the K/C class. It has no released code (FACT, per the baseline-roles document).

### Structural reason novelty fails (DERIVATION)

- At R ≥ 16, A3+A4 spans the whole positive passive cone, and w and u are identifiable only as their product. "Conditional angular-spatial operator" is therefore an output head, not a structural prior.
- Decoder dichotomy (systems):
  - A nonnegative linear decoder over fixed atoms is a higher-rank global ATO, which is not new.
  - A nonlinear decoder carries an apply cost of about 81× per pair.

### What might remain open (weak negative; HYPOTHESIS)

No work was found that combines all of the following:
- geometry- and pair-conditioned nonnegative low-rank angular blocks;
- a passive port × bin boundary operator for a bounded 3D scattering medium;
- exact D4h averaging;
- evaluation on held-out boundary sources from 16 one-hot supervised sources.

This is a combination, and searches were limited. It cannot carry a claim without a measured result. Its only element new relative to WL57–80, pair-conditioned angular factors (A3), targets a factor the record ranks as non-binding: WL75 l.308 and BND4_FAR 0.159 vs 0.80 (FACT, re-checked by the chair).

## Final Track A verdict

## Track A final verdict: BGC-Transport REJECTED

**Was the original BGC logically defensible?** Only as a passive parametrisation of K. It was not defensible as a mechanism for source generalisation.

**What is valid by construction (DERIVATION; not falsified):**
- per-input passivity;
- linearity, provided z depends on geometry only;
- positivity;
- exact per-pair nonnegative representability at R = 16;
- a physically motivated, distance-dependent pair rank.

**Why the distinguishing claim fails.** The claim is that pair-conditioned angular-spatial factors, generated from first-flight and relational encoders under normalisation, yield held-out transfer. It is unsupported on four counts:
1. **No identification (DERIVATION).** The hard constraints leave out-of-orbit boundary and deformed contexts unidentified, as the bump construction (CE-1) shows. BGC also has no structural mechanism that shares information across sources: no source-independent Q, and no reciprocity, which its independent ρ excludes.
2. **Wrong target (FACT, re-checked).** The only new element, A3, acts on the factor the record calls non-binding: WL75 l.195 (BND4_FAR 0.159 oracle vs 0.803 / 0.824 learned) and l.308.
3. **Already tested (FACT).** The remaining parts re-assemble mechanisms that have been built and tested:
   - the FIA normalisation, tail and anchor skeleton (WL76, INSUFFICIENT FIT);
   - the W+UV-Q / RSK relational inputs (WL78 INCONCLUSIVE; WL72 no gain);
   - the rest D4h maps (WL79/80).
4. **Structurally wrong normalisation (DERIVATION).** An independent ρ duplicates ΣK. It produces a receiver-uniform edit artefact (CE-2) and makes exact reciprocity impossible. The bound d is too loose; it should be ω·d.

**What survived (record-only corrections; not an architecture):**
- **C1.** Deterministic analytic-chord budget d, shared with B, with collision probability dropped. Benefit: removes noise and resampling error. It is not a speed gain.
- **C2.** Passive operator d·ω_c·χ_D·m·z/max(1,S): disjoint local/far supports, one shared denominator, ρ and ε removed. The projection must be predeclared and its binding fraction reported.
- **C3.** Gauge removal: w merged into u, v normalised.
- **C4.** Relational-input hygiene: clipping, tie-inclusive radius sets, a geometric frame, no rest coordinates, a declared σ_t law, and a corridor descriptor that is still unspecified.
- **C5.** Rest-only D4h, used as training augmentation and a predeclared test.

**What changed in the record.**
- Five amendments from the independent checker are incorporated:
  - the reciprocity condition is d_i·z_ij symmetric (z = d_j·s with s symmetric), plus a declared 16↔64 bin map;
  - the C1 cost claim is restated against the existing ~0.015 s β quadrature, not the 74.3 s SS/W quadrature;
  - d and B share one β;
  - FIA's anchor is described accurately: SS plus W at the second collision;
  - the symmetric form is renamed E_sym.
- Two reviewer errors are corrected: Adversary 1's "+200 %" second-order closure (the correct values are +10.4 / +21.8 / −17.6 %), and Adversary 2's "reciprocity pins nothing" (it pins mostly far-field entries).

**What was removed.** A3, the pair-conditioned free angular factors, and with it the rationale for the A6 pair-factor cache. The independent ρ and ε were also removed.

**New limitations introduced by the corrections:**
- The max(1,·) projection is non-smooth. Where it binds, coupling and non-reciprocity return. It has the form of a clamp, so it may only be predeclared.
- The corridor descriptor adds input dimension and memorisation capacity, and costs roughly 10⁷ queries per rebuild (unmeasured).
- Clipping at r_c may hide a second edge (TBE-far).
- The geometric frame is undefined at symmetric points.
- D4h augmentation risks orbit over-weighting (cf. WL80's 2–18 % original-orientation cost).
- The per-class rank is a hyperparameter that must be frozen first.
- The chord grid has its own discretisation error.
- The smooth local/far window adds a second length scale.
- The selective-recompute invalidation set still dilates by about 13–24 mm, and the Geometry+B rebuild already dominates (WL59: 0.32 of 0.33 s, FACT).

**Is the revision materially better?** It is better for validity: passivity bound, measure, single budget, removal of the ρ artefact, leak hygiene and honest scope. It is **not better for unseen-source transfer**, and it makes no such claim. No correction adds identification for out-of-orbit or deformed contexts.

**What remains unproven or unmeasured:**
- per-pair nonnegative angular rank at target accuracy by pair class (near, edge/corner, far) against a replica noise floor; this decides the cost and compressibility question;
- the actual cost and accuracy of A6 and of any fused apply;
- whether any generator's inductive bias happens to transfer, which is empirically unmeasured for BGC;
- the size of the CE-2 to CE-5 effects, all of which are SPECULATION or HYPOTHESIS;
- whether diffusion screening holds near edges and corners;
- the size of the reciprocity benefit;
- the σ_t law under deformation.

**Unresolved objections preserved:** identification (fatal), reciprocity construction, A1 insufficiency, corridor specification, A6 feasibility, rank R, selective-recompute dilation, the procedural clamp condition, and the σ_t law.

**Successors.**
- (R-a) FIA-family exact first-collision anchoring, (R-b) the symmetric Green's kernel via E_sym, and (R-c) higher-rank ATO are different architectures. None is adopted or recommended; each would need its own user decision.
- No implementation, training, batch, successor or adoption is authorised by this record.
- Any future cost comparison must pass the mandatory baseline gate in `docs/BASELINE_ROLES_AND_EVIDENCE_STRATEGY.md`, where Neural Full Recompute is not Cycles.

**Confidence:**
- The rejection of the mechanism claim: HIGH. It rests on derivations plus re-checked FACT anchors from several independent reviewers, and the checker concurred.
- The element-level corrections: MEDIUM. They are record-only, unbuilt, and one was already found erroneous and amended.
- Every cost or magnitude figure: LOW to MEDIUM. They are order-of-magnitude derivations and inferences, with no BGC instance measured.
