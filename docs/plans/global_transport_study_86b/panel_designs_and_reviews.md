## Binding constraints
- Exactly one owner per path segment or path class, including between additive learned terms: jointly fitted additive terms without separately identified targets compensate for each other (K_glob carried 86 % of the Yellow mode, WL51; ECRO remainder +0.02 to +0.06, WL86A).
- Anything applied recursively needs passivity certified on the whole queried domain (closed hemisphere, unsupervised ports and directions); linearity plus non-negative coefficients is not enough (WL41/42/46/50; WL81).
- The supervision target must be the operator the composition applies at that path position, taken from a path-history-independent, qualified reference (WL51, WL53, WL83 -> WL84).
- A fixed piecewise-constant angular interface cannot carry sharp lobes (WL58: 52-80 % loss at alpha 0.04; WL86A identical-encoding beam pairs differ 0.62-0.94, post hoc). A sharp sender's contribution has to be placed by sampling or integrating the sender's lobe, not by receiver-side lobe-independent gathering (WL46, WL48 KF, WL86A amendment 2; Veach Theorem 8.3).
- Evaluation must be per path family and reader-importance weighted; aggregate field error, flux conservation, whole-image PSNR and first-event fidelity did not predict composed or sharp-receiver behaviour (WL42 -> WL44/45, WL49 -> WL50, WL86 -> WL86A).
- Representability of the family (direct fit / oracle) must be shown before a generator is trained (WL81 rule), and oracle existence is not evidence that a geometry-conditioned rule can produce it (WL71-75: oracle 0.13-0.17, learned transfer 0.79-0.82).
- A learned object that takes scene geometry as input must beat the learning-free operator built from the same geometry state, and the whole mechanism must beat quality-matched path tracing: explicit C update 5-11 ms (WL52); Cycles 4 spp 4.7 s versus 190 s oracle update (WL67); cold frozen-ER path tracing reaches the ER-gap level in 0.39 s GPU on the WL85 fixture.
- On sharp receivers fidelity to REF-ER is bounded in meaning by ER's own gap g of 0.059-0.065, the same size as the WL86 gate (0.06).
- Rigid-first designs must state what happens under deformation: intrinsic change is not generally local or low-rank (WL53 bend rank90 133-151), and a stale K recovers 1-20 % of a receiver's change (WL56).
- Port- or patch-discretised K/C interfaces need sub-port resolution on both sides near contact (WL54/55). This binds discretised interfaces, not continuous ones.
- Uncertain rather than binding: whether passivity requirements transfer to one-shot (non-recursive) learned operators; whether the WL57-80 transfer failures are properties of the model class or of training scale and label noise.

### HYPOTHESIS: H1 - Intrinsic-only learning: K as a certified, sampleable first-exit closure; coupling and the multi-bounce solve stay physically computed and are sampled from K; no learned global operator [defensible]
- central_principle: In b = K(e + C_G b) exactly one factor is at once rigid-invariant in material coordinates, source-independent by physics, and expensive to compute physically: K. C_G is a cheap deterministic function of geometry, and the cost of the solve is a sampling cost, not a missing function. [inf from meas] Every learned object that took scene geometry as an input failed on transfer or on economics against explicit geometry (learned C, WL52; geometry -> spatial factor, WL57-80; ECRO generator, WL86 sharp class), and the one learned object that met its gates (ER, WL82-84) takes none. The hypothesis is therefore that learning is confined to K; K is exposed to the rest of the system only through a transition-kernel contract (eval, sample, pdf, certified passivity); and angular support for sharp transport is obtained by sampling C from K's outgoing distribution on the sender side, so that support follows the material and not a fixed ray or bin budget. Under this hypothesis GM1 has no learned global object, and the surface-only stage is an interface and reference qualification stage for the K contr
- primary_operator_math: (K_a i)(x_o, w_o) = double integral of k_a(x_o, w_o | x_i, w_i; material, intrinsic geometry) i(x_i, w_i) cos(theta_i) dw_i dA_i, in material coordinates; k_a >= 0; sup over the closed incident hemisphere of the integral of k_a cos(theta_o) dw_o dA_o <= 1 (a certificate, not a penalty); k_a = sum over a finite set of lobes k_l, each with exact sample and pdf. Surfaces: k = f_ER(w_i, w_o) delta(x_o - x_i). (C_G b)(x, w) = b(r_G(x, -w), w): a ray cast in the current geometry. T_G = K C_G, b = sum_n T_G^n K e, evaluated by a walk whose scattering transitions are drawn from k and whose flights are ray casts; measurement through the adjoint walk from the camera. No operator other than K has learned parameters. Any stored state is defined as a projection of the physically computed field and assigned to path classes by K's lobe index.
- what_is_learned: The compiler from material (and, at GM2, intrinsic local geometry in material coordinates) to closure parameters. Today: the frozen ER compiler (150,848 parameters) producing a 69-float passive, reciprocal state per material [impl]. At GM2 [hyp]: the same contract with an exit-position distribution (x_o different from x_i), i.e. a learned first-exit transition kernel for a medium or transmissive domain.
- what_is_physically_computed: C_G (acceleration structure and ray casts in the current geometry), the source field, every flight and every composition of K with C, the ballistic term, and the camera readout. Any reuse across frames or edits is physical state with explicit geometric dependencies.
- persistent_vs_edit_dependent_state: Persistent across all supported rigid edits: compiler weights and per-domain closure states, moved with the domain's frame. Edit-dependent: acceleration structure (2.5 ms in-place edit measured in WL85), any cached relation or radiance whose line set the mover enters or leaves, and the solved radiance. Under a non-rigid edit the deformed domain's K state is invalid and is regenerated by the compiler from the new intrinsic geometry (GM3; untested).
- path_ownership_and_composition: Segment ownership exactly as in Architecture section 2: K owns entry to first exit, C owns exit to next entry. Because the walk alternates one K transition and one C ray cast, a segment cannot be claimed twice. Stability follows from certified sub-stochastic transitions (Neumann series converges with ratio at most the maximum albedo); nothing learned is ever queried outside its certificate. The same K is used for direct and coupled incidence, which ECRO's internal rank-16 scattering did not satisfy.
- source_of_compressibility: Physical and structural, and confined to K. (1) Intrinsic micro-transport - a layered-coat walk, an in-medium walk with many collisions - collapses to a few lobes or a low-dimensional profile because of local material statistics (ER: 8 lobes; WL66: 3-5 incoming contrast directions for a homogeneous slab). (2) Reuse multiplicity: the same K is evaluated at every vertex of every path in every frame under every light and every rigid edit, with zero invalidation. No compressibility is claimed for T_G or its resolvent, which is non-compact (Soler et al. 2022, survey).
- cost_model: Offline supervision: N_materials x c_reference_eval (ER: 3,000 materials); at GM2 N_shapes x photons, the measured expensive item (126 M photons, 380 s, 2.0 GB per 10x10x1 cm slab at 2.5 mm; x4 per halving of cell size). Geometry state: build O(n log n), refit O(n_moved). Relation discovery: none stored. Learned-state generation: O(number of domains) compiler evaluations when a material or intrinsic shape changes; zero per rigid edit. Source application: none separate. Per edit: refit only. Per frame: pixels x spp x mean path length x (c_trace + c_K). Memory: O(domains x closure size) plus the acceleration structure. Directional complexity enters as variance: spp grows for caustic-type families; worst case is sharp chains with small lights, where light-side sampling or manifold connections are needed and cost grows with chain length. Amortisation base is every scene that uses the material family, not one scene family. Measured anchors: ER versus layered reference 44-10,600x per evaluation, 1.45-1.63x per spp in WL84, 2.9x in WL85; cold frozen-ER path tracing 0.73 ms GPU per spp at 51
- is_saving_unique_to_learning: Partly. The K saving exists for any compact closure; a physical representation with the same role is a tabulated or precomputed operator (WL55 oracle K: 2 GB and 380 s per domain instance). What learning adds is the generator: a new material or shape gets its closure without per-instance precomputation (WL83). At GM1 the global saving is zero by construction, and any reuse added on top is physical and published (WL84A: all three reuse tracks withdrawn by their own advocates).

### HYPOTHESIS: H2 - Enclosure-bounded intrinsic operator: extend the optical domain to a rigid assembly including its enclosed air (a contract amendment) [weakly_defensible]
- central_principle: For any closed virtual interface Gamma around a rigid sub-scene whose interior does not change, the interior's response to the light field incident on Gamma is an exact linear, passive, source-independent, rigid-invariant operator (the interior unknowns eliminated: a Schur complement of the transport system). For a medium, Gamma is the material boundary and this is the contract's K. The hypothesis is that the same object, with Gamma allowed to enclose air (a concave glossy assembly), is the nonlocal intrinsic thing that can be learned at the surface-only stage, in the same sampleable-kernel form as H1.
- primary_operator_math: K_Gamma = R_out (I - T_int)^-1 T_in on the 4D ray space of Gamma, where T_int = K C restricted to the interior, T_in maps rays entering Gamma to their first interior event and R_out maps interior exits to rays leaving Gamma. Outside: b = K_Gamma(e + C_ext b) with C_ext owning only segments outside every Gamma. Represented as a transition kernel k_Gamma(ray_out | ray_in) with sample / pdf and a sub-stochastic certificate.
- what_is_learned: A compiler from the assembly's intrinsic geometry and materials to k_Gamma (per rigid assembly, in its frame). [hyp] Nothing of this kind exists in the project.
- what_is_physically_computed: All transport outside the enclosures, the hull-intrusion test that certifies validity, and the fallback (plain K and C inside Gamma) whenever foreign geometry enters the hull or the assembly deforms.
- persistent_vs_edit_dependent_state: Persistent under rigid motion of the whole assembly while its hull is not intruded. Invalid (fall back or regenerate) under hull intrusion, near contact, or any internal non-rigid edit.
- path_ownership_and_composition: Segments inside Gamma belong to K_Gamma, segments outside to C_ext; exclusive if hulls are disjoint. - source_of_compressibility: Physical: an interior multi-bounce chain is replaced by one transition. Statistical only where the exit distribution is smooth; for sharp interior transport there is none (space-bandwidth bound).

### HYPOTHESIS: H3 - Learned broad-class resolvent as an optional approximation of the physical solve (a restricted ECRO), with ownership by K's lobe classes [not_defensible_but_informative]

Every family with a narrow-lobe sender feeding a stored vertex goes to a sender-driven physical estimator; narrow-lobe reads at the camera side are traced. Ownership is by lobe index of ER's exact mixture, applied identically on both sides.
- primary_operator_math: Write S_ER = S_B + S_N (broad and narrow lobes by a declared r50 threshold). - what_is_learned: The generator G_theta from geometry state to operator factors, for one declared scene family and material family. Its parameters are scene-family state, not an intrinsic rule.
- what_is_physically_computed: Low-order paths, all narrow-lobe sender families, the geometry state (rays), the source encoding, the readout.
- persistent_vs_edit_dependent_state: Persistent: generator weights within the trained family. Edit-dependent: state rays, generated factors and table, all regenerated in full on every edit (as in ECRO [impl]).

- source_of_compressibility: Physical band-limit of broad-to-broad transport (low space-bandwidth product). The same band-limit is what learning-free caches exploit.

## Strongest counterargument to own best
H1 can be read as giving up the research objective at the stage where it is currently being tested. The project's invariant is a learned transport response that stays valid under edits and is cheaper than full physical transport; under H1 the surface-only method is frozen-ER path tracing, which the roadmap says does not complete GM1 and which the topic document excludes as a contribution ('a fast BSDF surrogate'). Its measured economics are modest: 1.45-2.9x per sample and no wall-clock gain. The learned content that would make H1 more than that is deferred to GM2, and that is exactly the object with the worst record in the project: across WL57-80 no learned nonlocal K met a transfer or deformation gate, and exact oracle state mechanisms lost to Cycles by 10-40x on small fixtures. H1 also rests on an untested inference - that ECRO's broad-region result (0.003-0.006 at 5.45x cheaper samples) is equally available to a physical operator on the same state. If the learned operator in fact acts as a useful regulariser there, H1 discards a real lever. Finally, the generalisation 'geometry-conditioned learning fails' is drawn from three mechanisms trained on small distributions (one room, one slab, one synthetic family); large cross-scene models in the survey do transfer to edited geometry at moderate quality, so the failures may reflect training scale and not a principle.

## Overall assessment
Nothing is selected here.

Where learning has worked [meas]: only in K, and only in its pointwise form. ER is the single learned object that passed representability, generalisation and rendering gates; it takes no scene geometry and is passive and reciprocal by construction. Learned objects that take scene geometry as input have a uniform record: valid and stable when built that way, but either uncompetitive with explicit geometry (learned C: 44-672 ms against 5-11 ms) or not transferring (intrinsic spatial map: 0.79-0.82 against oracles of 0.16-0.27; ECRO: sharp-sender family 1-18 % delivered). Physically computed state mechanisms met most of their correctness gates and lost on economics to quality-matched path tracing on small fixtures.

Where ECRO sits [inf]: beside the K/C contract. Ownership by event count is exclusive, but its learned parameters are scene-family state rather than an intrinsic rule, it substitutes a one-shot operator for 'recompute through the current coupled solve', and it carries a second, binned representation of the local scattering that ER already owns. For later milestones this matters concretely: a shared patch x bin scattering matrix assumes exit point equals entry point (false for transmission and subsurface transport at GM2), every generated factor depends on all geometry so nothing is selectively preserved (GM3), and a new scene family means retraining (GM4 lifecycle).

## Binding constraints
- Each path has one owner, and this must hold among learned terms, not only between the learned part and the traced part. Terms trained on a sum with overlapping function spaces are not separately identified; ownership then has to be enforced by separately tallied targets (path tagging costs nothing in the reference tracer; WL63 and WL86A already did it).
- A learned term's inputs must be a sufficient statistic for its own target over the declared source class, at the granularity the reader needs. This is measurable before training as the variance of the target among inputs with identical encodings (WL47 equal-irradiance pairs: outputs differ 0.98-1.11 at delta-E <= 5e-6; WL86A beam pairs).
- Every quantity the composition reads, including internal intermediates and unsupervised exit directions, is either supervised or bounded by construction (WL45, WL46, WL47 B0: hidden outputs wrong 1.03-1.06 and non-passive 1.77; WL86A Bq).
- A fixed piecewise-constant direction interface cannot carry a lobe narrower than its bin (WL58: 52-80 % loss at alpha 0.04 with 64/256 bins; two inputs in one bin force >= 56 % error). Applies to any fixed-basis table, learned or not.
- A sharp sender illuminated by a small or delta source cannot be found by a receiver-side ray set chosen before the source is known, at budgets that scale as the inverse lobe solid angle per receiver (Veach 8.3 in the prior-art file; consistent with the WL86A state-relation measurement).
- Aggregate norms (field relative L1, flux, whole-image PSNR, absolute-action norms) certify neither sharp reads, nor directional contrast, nor edit response (WL42 -> WL44/45; WL57-63; WL86 -> WL86A).
- A recursively applied learned operator must be passive on the fields and exit directions the composition actually produces (WL41/42/46/50). For a one-shot operator this is not established as necessary (ECRO), but out-of-distribution sources are then a bounded-bias risk.
- The supervision target must be the operator the composition applies at that path position, from a path-history-independent reference (WL51, WL53).
- A source representation averaged over more than about half the near-field distance loses contact and visibility-transition transport, learned or not (WL52, WL54, WL55).
- A learned approximation only adds value on an edit if its error is below the edit-induced change (WL57/59), and the whole mechanism only adds value if edit-to-valid-output cost is below quality-matched path tracing (WL85: 0.39 s GPU to reach g; WL67/69/70: 1.2-4.7 s) and below an explicit GPU coupling update of ~5-11 ms (WL52).
- Selective state update is not where savings are when the state rebuild is already milliseconds (WL86: 21 against 26-28 ms wall; WL59: 1.4-1.7 %).

### HYPOTHESIS: H1. Bandwidth-matched class operator: a learned response restricted to broad-only path runs, with every sharp-lobe event carried as a sampled flight on the side that can sample it [weakly_defensible]
- central_principle: A learned operator with a finite patch x bin interface is given exactly the path class for which that interface is a sufficient statistic: runs of broad-lobe scattering events. Sharp-lobe events are never tabulated and never learned; each is carried at ray precision by the side that can sample it with bounded variance (source side when the far end is a small source, lobe-sampled continuation otherwise). - primary_operator_math: S_ER = S_B + S_S lobe-wise (threshold on the lobe's width at the actual incidence, tied to the bin width). - Whether the -20 % aggregate deficit of exact panel radiance read through the deployed rays is a bias of the shared stratification or sampling noise. Only one ray realisation exists. - A source-side (sender-anchored) estimate of the light -> panel -> Lambert patch family at equal ray count, scored per patch against the path-traced family. My prediction of <= 10 % patch error with ~10^4 rays is arithmetic only; if it fails, the sampleability argument for the partition fails with it.
- Equal-encoding variance of each class target at patch level, for point, cone and narrow-beam sources. The existing encoding-equivalence test covers one member of the class, three point-light configurations and a region-level signed statistic; the beam evidence is post hoc and its statistic has no script in the repository.
- A retraining that changes one factor at a time (class-tagged targets, supervised sender radiance Bq, importance-weighted loss, denser state). WL86A did none, so sampling limit and learning failure are not separated. This matters only if a learned sharp flight is to be kept at all.
- An accuracy-against-cost ladder at the g-anchored quality level with an optimised comparator (Russian roulette, path-traced table, uncontended kernel times). Both arms in WL86 stopped short of g, and the protocol verdict is NOT TESTED.

- Edit response on sharp receivers: region C change slopes of 0.36-0.63 are stored, ungated and not decomposed by path family, so it is unknown whether the dynamic failure is the same family as the static one.
- Scaling regimes in which a source-independent operator could amortise at all (many lights, many views, larger N_p with the generator's quadratic message passing) have never been measured in either direction.
## Overall assessment
From this lens the evidence does not yet support selecting any learned global mechanism; nothing here is selected.

CONTRADICTED. A learned flight from a sharp sender through a receiver-side, source-independent ray relation and a fixed bin interface (ECRO v0's single-flight term). The measured state-relation result, the counting argument that reproduces its size, WL58's analytic bin limit and the prior art on gathering against shooting agree. Also contradicted: any design in which learned terms share a summed target and are expected to sort themselves into path families (the four-term K, ECRO's two terms).

WEAKENED. ECRO v0 as a whole. Its container and its broad-transport behaviour stand (oracle at noise; 0.003-0.006 on broad regions), but its measured advantage is a cache effect that a physically filled table shares; the explicit comparator was never timed; its broad generalisation is less clean than the aggregate gate suggested (unseen box patches 1.6-2.7x worse); and by my arithmetic an unbiased use of the table would keep only about 1.2x of the 9x. A source-independent compressed composed system is weakened as a general object, because sufficiency of its encoding holds for point lights and fails for narrow sources.

DEFENSIBLE. The partition principle: assign a finite-interface term only the path class on which its interface is a sufficient statistic, defined lobe-wise (exact for ER), and carry sharp events on the side that can sample them. This is defensible as a constraint on any future design, learned or not, and is testable without training. H2 (no learned global mechanism; learning stays in local closures) is defensible as the position the evidence currently supports, with no novelty and with an unresolved admissibility question in the central documents.

WEAKLY DEFENSIBLE. H1's learned component, a learned tail of the broad extended resolvent. Its target is identifiable by construction, which no earlier learned global term could claim, but the same smoothness that makes it identifiable makes it cheap to compute, and on sharp-dominated material populations it may own almost nothing.

WHAT WOULD DISTINGUISH A HYPOTHESIS FROM ANOTHER HEURISTIC DECOMPOSITION. Five checks, all executable before any training: (1) each term's path class is tallied separately in the reference and the tallies sum to it; (2) the equal-encoding variance of each learned term

### HYPOTHESIS: H2. No learned global operator: explicit lobe-partitioned transport on the geometry state, with learning kept in local closures [defensible]

## Defensible
Four positions survive the reviews, none of them as a selected architecture.

1. **The partition identity, as a constraint.** Splitting ER lobe-wise into a broad and a narrow set gives an exact, exclusive partition of path space: light-side narrow prefix, broad core, eye-side narrow suffix. ## Heuristic-decomposition risk
The survivors carry two different risks. The null is not a decomposition under a new name; it is a classical renderer under a new name, and its risk is being treated as a candidate. The partition-plus-learned-tail hypothesis carries the heuristic-decomposition risk in full.

- **The boundary is a convention drawn around the measured failure.** The lobe sum is exact, but which lobes count as narrow is a threshold.
