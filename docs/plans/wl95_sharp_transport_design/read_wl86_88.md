## Common metric definitions

- [FACT] **E_H(X)** = Σ|hl_X − h_REF-ER| / Σ tot_REF-ER over region X. It is the extra error of the higher-order term relative to *total* radiance. The LOW estimator is shared with the reference, so LOW noise is excluded. **H share** = E_H of LOW alone. **capture** = E_H / H share (`experiments/ecro86/protocol/ecro86.json:90`; WL86:56).
- [FACT] WL86 gates (`ecro86.json:97-105`):
  - F1: stationary E_H ≤ 0.03 and capture ≤ 0.25, at T0 and T1.
  - F2: E_H ≤ 0.06 in regions A, C and D at T1, for E1 and E2.
  - F3: slope of the higher-order change dH in D within 0.75–1.25, correlation ≥ 0.8.
  - F4: unseen sources (LMIX, LSPOT) stationary E_H ≤ 0.04.
  - F5: STALE control, reported only.
  - Oracle gate R1: stationary ≤ 0.02, regions ≤ 0.04, capture ≤ 0.25 (`ecro86.json:88,93`).
- [FACT] Regions are built from geometry and visibility only (WL86:51):
  - A: direct visibility changes.
  - C: mirror footprint enabled or blocked, or panel / sphere receivers whose mirror cone sees a change.
  - D: broad receivers with unchanged direct visibility.
  - E: exposure ≤ 1 %.
- [FACT] **g** = the gap between REF-ER and REF-P (frozen-ER versus physical layered material) on stationary pixels. It is reported apart from the method error (WL86:147).
- [FACT] **P** (WL86A) = importance-weighted signed error of a table in a region, as a share of total radiance (WL86A:110).

## 1. ECRO v0 (WL86): design and rationale

**Hypothesis** [FACT] (WL86:7): a compact, source-independent, geometry-conditioned learned *linear* operator can:
- predict the higher-order directional transport generated by frozen ER and the current geometry;
- generalise to unseen lights and rigid edits;
- beat full frozen-ER path tracing at matched quality.

**Contract** [FACT] (WL86:17-21):
- T = S_ER C_G, q = S_ER e, truncation at D = 6 events.
- LOW = q + Tq is path-traced.
- b̂ = q + Tq + T·R_θ(G, z_ER)[e].
- The last flight T reuses the x1 → x2 bounce that LOW traces anyway. The camera reads `throughput(x1) × B[patch(x2), bin(x2→x1)]` and casts no extra ray. The table therefore supplies events 3–6.

**Ownership** [FACT] (WL86:25-27):
- ER owns every physically evaluated scattering event.
- Geometry owns visibility.
- ECRO owns only paths with ≥ 3 events. B excludes e2, so nothing is counted twice.

**Representation** [FACT] (WL86:31-34):
- 1,270 patches × 64 equal-solid-angle bins (4 cos θ rings × 16 azimuth sectors; WL86A:16).
- Source encoding s [1270, 64, 3] = patch-average direct irradiance per incident bin, from 16 points per patch per light.
- Output table B [1270, 64, 3] = outgoing radiance of 2–5 events.
- State = 512 rays per patch, 8 per incident bin, storing hit patch and outgoing bin. Selective re-trace after an edit.
- WL87:31,35 adds that one offset draw (`default_rng(86001)`) is shared by *all* patches, so every patch uses the same 512 local directions.

**Operator** [FACT] (WL86:38-41; `experiments/ecro86/model86.py`):
- 0.74 M parameters (735,664). The generator never sees radiance.
- 3 rounds of message passing over the state-ray form-factor matrix produce non-negative factors: c (incident bin → R = 16 channels), a (channels → outgoing bin), and a rank-24 global encoder / decoder P, Q.
- Application: u = c·s; single learned flight E1 = Σ_rays (a·u)[hit, bin] cos dΩ; B = a·(c·E1 + P·Σ area_j Q_j u_j).
- Linear, non-negative and superposition-exact by construction. No passivity constraint on the multi-flight response.

**Supervision** [FACT] (WL86:43, 129):
- Path-traced 2–5-event field per patch and bin.
- 1,024 training geometries × 4 white point lights, targets at 256 spp (target noise 0.10 relative L1).
- Test geometries and lights are excluded with margins. No image supervision.

**Why this design** [INF] from WL86:251 and WL86B:25:
- Physical LOW supplies about 76 % of the radiance and the sharp edit response.
- The table replaces path-tracer bounces 3–6 with one lookup.
- Learning is intended to remove the per-light physical fill, giving source independence and zero-lag validity (WL86B:163).

## 3. Design study (WL86B): no implementation; Korean report

**Main conclusion** [FACT] (WL86B:16, 215-220): no learned global architecture can be selected on current evidence. Two hypotheses remain conditionally defensible, plus a null. The decisive evidence is all learning-free and absent.

**Refuted forms** [MEAS-cited] (WL86B:18-21):
- Dense learned coupling (WL52, cost).
- Learned K fitted to non-passive or path-history-dependent targets and recursed.
- Additive learned decompositions supervised only on their sum: the four-part K, and ECRO's single flight + global term.

**Correction of an earlier inference** [MEAS] (WL86B:27): "sharp failure = structural receiver-side gather" was weakened by the 74–84 % (relation) versus 1–18 % (learned) contrast. Most of the failure is unseparated learning failure.

**Structural observations** [INF] (WL86B:44-48):
- **(i)** Reuse has two meanings. State reuse keeps K and valid links. Rule reuse lets a fixed rule regenerate the whole state. ECRO is rule reuse.
- **(ii)** The resolvent (I − T_G)⁻¹ has no edit locality. [MEAS] Selective update was worth only 20.8 against 28.1 ms.
- **(iii)** Sharp directional relations live only in S's narrow lobes and in C's visibility.
- **(iv)** In GM1 every transport step is explicitly cheap. Only variance and path length are expensive.
- **(v)** [FACT] ECRO partitions by event count, not by K/C segment. No canonical document relates ECRO to the K/C contract.

**Common failure pattern of four-part K and ECRO** [INF] (WL86B:66-78):
- The outer partition is exclusive, but the inner additive learned terms are supervised only on their sum.
- The geometry-poor term absorbs the geometry-specific term's deficit.
- Aggregate metrics (field error, flux, whole image) do not predict behaviour under another reader.

**Hypotheses** [HYP]:
- **H-P** (WL86B:93-110): lobe-class exclusive partition S = S_B + S_N, physical narrow prefix and suffix, learned broad tail only. Panel score: WEAKENED 2 / REFUTED 1.
  - The broad → narrow → broad link has no visibility.
  - Moderate lobes have no owner.
  - The class boundary sits post hoc next to the panel: r50 0.146 rad against 0.15.
- **H-0** (WL86B:114-117): null, explicit patch × bin transport on the same state. Not novel. Whether it is a candidate or a baseline is the owner's decision.
- **H-E** (WL86B:121-124): the ECRO form is kept and the *recipe* is the cause. Candidate causes, never changed one at a time:
  - per-term targets;
  - Bq sender supervision;
  - importance-weighted loss;
  - denser state;
  - larger R.

  It is decisively refuted if the global term still compensates after per-term targets, or if the non-negative rank of the panel matrix ≫ 16.
- Five others were rejected (WL86B:128-134).

**Economics** [INF] (WL86B:151):
- The readout saving is not unique to learning.
- The fill advantage of learned over explicit is bounded by J (number of sweeps, about 3–5), while the generator is O(N²).
- The only learning-unique gain would be accuracy on a coarse basis (sub-patch / sub-bin closure). It is unmeasured.

**Counterarguments** [MEAS/INF] (WL86B:169-186):
- 89 % of what region C reads comes from Lambert senders; glossy-to-glossy chains are at most 11 %.
- No edit has a sharp mover.
- Point lights make the sharp-sender family hard.
- Fidelity was gated at converged quality, but economics at noise quality, where path tracing's own C error (0.12–0.32) exceeds ECRO's bias (about 0.07).

**Evidence gaps** [FACT] (WL86B:194-203), the decisive ones learning-free:
- #1 explicit transport on the same state;
- #2 path-traced-table and Russian-roulette ladders;
- #3 repeated RAY / BIN tests;
- #4 rank and non-negative rank of the 64×64 ER matrices;
- #5 lobe-tagged change;
- #6 one-factor ECRO retrains, whose permission is the owner's call under the no-rescue rule;
- #7 sharp-mover oracle;
- #8 a workload where STALE fails in broad regions.

**Admissibility criteria for a decomposition** [FACT] (WL86B:222-227): class rule fixed prospectively from a measured quantity; class tallies sum to the reference; a falsifiable forward prediction; oracle representability before training; per-term tallied targets with reader-importance-weighted family scores.
