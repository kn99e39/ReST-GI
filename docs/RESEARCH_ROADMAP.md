## Current frontier

The approved research milestone is **GM1** (below), with ECRO as the learned-global-transport implementation base and the learned ER closure as the local-scattering baseline. The dedicated Certified ER fidelity investigation is closed. Every further batch needs its own human decision.

### Deferred validation — not a milestone or authorised batch

- **Material-distribution transfer (deferred falsification question).** An ECRO model trained on a near-mirror panel, evaluated without retraining on the original sharp-panel material, showed promising system-level robustness. How much of that apparent transfer comes from physically recomputed LOW versus genuinely reusable learned HIGH, and how much from the direction of the sharp-to-rough material shift? With frozen weights and unchanged geometry/light/readout controls, compare trained near-mirror and original conditions using independent physical LOW/HIGH references, separate LOW/HIGH/full errors, a fixed reflection ROI, uncertainty-qualified sampling and a declared roughness-shift test. Do not infer simultaneous geometry + material-edit coverage from it.

**Retained local-scattering decision (human, 2026-11-10):** The learned ER closure is the **primary development baseline for local material scattering**, not the selected final ReST-GI architecture. Its demonstrated scope is a coated-conductor family, learned descriptor-to-closure generalisation, local physical/runtime contracts and limited static rendering/economics. Dynamic interreflection, learned nonlocal intrinsic response and quality-matched end-to-end dynamic GI remain **UNTESTED with this baseline**. [Architecture](Architecture.md) owns the baseline contract. The approved *research milestone* is GM1; **no implementation batch or later milestone is automatically authorised**.

### Global development milestones — approved direction, not approved implementation batches

The sequence below defines **questions, dependencies, controls and exit decisions**, not a predetermined solver, learned C, spatial data structure or final architecture. A milestone can close negatively if its evidence is valid. Numerical gates, scenes, representation hypothesis, hardware accounting and stop conditions are frozen in each separately authorised experiment *before* its primary results.

| Milestone | Central question and bounded scope | Evidence required for a decision | Exit / dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GM0 — local foundation** | Can one compact learned, physically admissible local scattering rule serve as the development starting point? | Direct-fit representation, learned generalisation and runtime contracts, qualified local rendering and measured costs. | **QUALIFIED for development**: learned **ER** primary; learned E lightweight control; neither is a nonlocal K or final architecture. No renewed local-closure search without new failure evidence. |
| **GM1 — dynamic directional interreflection** (**next**) | With frozen ER, can **sharp glossy/specular, multi-bounce, editable-visibility interreflection** be represented and updated under a **rigid occluder/object edit**, including changed radiance on stationary receivers? | At T0/T1, (1) original layered-BSDF physical path-traced reference; (2) full path tracing using the *same frozen ER* to isolate its local bias; (3) one explicit proposed global mechanism; (4) stale/no-update and same-mechanism full rebuild; (5) directional/highlight, signed-change, multi-bounce and visibility-affected/unaffected evidence; (6) all update/solve/readout/memory costs and a predeclared quality-matched edit-to-valid-output and per-frame accounting. No T1 supervision/oracle at runtime. | **Go** only if a valid directional global representation and edit response meet prospective quality and cost requirements; otherwise attribute representation / composition / update / economic failure before changing the hypothesis. Proving only an image match or implementing a full retracer does not complete GM1. |
| **GM2 — complex transport breadth** | Can the mechanism extend from the GM1 glossy case to **nonlocal material/interior response, transmission/SSS and heterogeneous multi-bounce paths** without double counting or losing spatial/angular support? | Independent physically qualified mixed-material scenes, first-exit and exposed-free-space path ownership, boundary-port and ballistic/scattered controls, local-vs-global error decomposition, static and edited fidelity plus cost on the same method. | Decide whether the same representation extends, needs a bounded compatible augmentation, or has an identified capacity/ownership limit. | **GM3 — edit generality and state validity** | Which parts of the transport state remain valid under **local deformation, global bending/twisting and self-contact/topology changes**, in addition to rigid motion? | Correspondence and dependency/invalidation evidence, edited-path coverage, negative stale-state controls, accuracy of unchanged and changed receivers, conservative/full fallback, total update and solver cost. | Define supported edit classes and explicit invalidate/rebuild boundaries; unsupported motion cannot be claimed. Depends on an eligible transport mechanism from GM1–GM2. |
| **GM4 — scalability and contribution qualification** | Does the supported method offer **quality-matched lifecycle advantage** on more complex scenes and repeated edits, including sufficiently accurate interreflection, compared with applicable prior art? | Scaling of relation discovery, angular state, memory, solve iterations and update locality; edit-to-valid-GI and sustained-frame cost including preparation/training amortisation; within-ReST-GI full rebuild and stale controls; physically aligned external **Neural Full Recompute** when actually invalidated, **Native Reuse** when valid, and separate path-traced re-render cost. Novelty claims must survive targeted prior-art comparison. | Decide final supported contribution and architecture viability (or a scoped negative). This is **not** permission to claim superiority before the comparisons run. |

**Across every milestone:** (i) fidelity **and** edit response **and** economics are evaluated together, not as isolated deferred optimisations; (ii) local ER approximation error must be distinguished from global transport/composition error; (iii) do not freeze a representation or selective-update algorithm prior to its mechanism hypothesis; (iv) preserve evidence of negative baselines and genuine full-vs-selective cost; (v) continue only after human interpretation/authorisation at each architecture decision. Rigid-first does **not** remove non-rigid or self-contact coverage from the final research objective.

## Current decision boundary

**Current decision (human, 2026-11-13):** ECRO is reinstated as the **central learned-global-transport implementation base** for GM1, while the learned ER closure remains the local-scattering baseline. The original ECRO v0 verdict remains NOT SUPPORTED under its frozen sharp/held-out gates: this is a starting-point selection, not adoption or a successful rerun. The explicit comparator relations and the sampling/projection diagnostics remain immutable null/comparator evidence, not a replacement for learned global transport. The next architecture decision must isolate **one falsifiable ECRO-lineage representation/training/ownership hypothesis** that addresses the measured learned-flight sharp deficit and compensation without sacrificing source-independent reuse, edited-geometry response or quality-matched economics. Fix the physical/angular interface, T0/T1 and held-out controls, independent references, matched explicit comparator and prospective gates before an authorised evidence run. Do not tune the original ECRO v0 result retrospectively, promote an explicit candidate as the project goal, or launch an automatic successor batch.

**Mandatory SSS near-contact correctness gate (owner direction 2026-11-24; independent of the external-baseline / contribution-approval track):** **NOT_RESOLVED.**
- *Required:* the SSS near-contact artifact must be resolved by actual learned ECRO, across independent cameras and changing contact configurations, without per-state retraining. Average quality, runtime or other material classes cannot compensate for it. Only RESOLVED is eligible for milestone approval.
- *Current blocking mechanism:* the spatial resolution of the higher-order output on the near-contact (lowest-ring) sphere patches. 2×2 sub-patches are insufficient under a qualified camera-independent construction. A 4×4 uniform table is sufficient post hoc on one fixture.
- *Open decision (owner):* adopting a finer or adaptive output representation would be a new hypothesis.
- *Update 2026-11-25 (WL113; gate still NOT_RESOLVED):*
  - **Oracle (physical construction).**
    - A camera-independent dense 4×4 uniform table removes the depth-matched artifact in independent configurations: all WL112 bands at all 14 contact-valid views.
    - The compact VHS ∪ MEDIUM support keeps the contact fidelity but not the sphere silhouette.
    - 4×4 still leaves a residual within about 10 cm of the support. Against the approved 3 % contract this fails the localisation and ring requirements (R1L, R4).
  - **Learned dense-4×4 ECRO.**
    - It reproduces the oracle at contact configurations, with exact rebuild and revisit.
    - It has a learned bias of about +0.01 to +0.016 at new positions and in lifted states.
  - **Full depth.** Every depth-6 representation fails the full-depth 3 % contract through truncation (−7 to −17 % in the near ROI).
  - **Remaining blockers:**
    - contact-scale resolution below 4×4;
    - learned generalisation at lifted / new positions;
    - transport depth.

Do not treat historical failures of the earlier intrinsic-K line as observed defects of the learned ER baseline. They are possible failure mechanisms and diagnostic controls. Do not convert the local ER success into a full-K, global-interreflection, dynamic-reuse or end-to-end superiority claim. GM2–GM4 are directional commitments, not automatically approved work.

## Longer-term decisions, without implementation permission

- Resolve the complete physical quality floor and the shared angular/deformation response limitations before claiming learned integration readiness.
- Revisit rigid motion, local deformation, global bending/twisting and self-contact at each major design decision. Rigid material-frame evidence is conditional; the tested learned local-deformation recipes fail their complete response contract; global deformation and contact generality remain unestablished.
- Consider interpretable dependency discovery and optional learned scoring only after physical coverage and representation feasibility are established, with algorithm-only and oracle controls.
- **Deferred real-scene stress-test candidate (human direction, 2026-11-16; NOT an adopted capability or authorised batch):** First make spatial-relation changes usable in a dynamic scene: verify quality-preserving edit/update correctness and practical performance under repeated rigid motions, reporting **edit-to-first-valid-GI latency, sustained end-to-end frame time, latency tails and memory separately**. The owner's provisional *frame* budgets are **15 ms target, 20 ms acceptable if the fidelity/utility trade-off warrants it, 10 ms optimal**; these are research targets, not demonstrated results or a frozen future gate. Only after that prerequisite is supported, test (1) articulated hard-surface mechs with moving rigid parts, changing inter-part visibility and sharp interreflection; then separately add (2) fog, (3) water and (4) fire/volumetric emission, followed by a combined visual-effects scene. Use physically qualified references, stale/full-update controls and matched-quality performance; preserve failure cases. Rigid-part motion is a direct transport-reuse stress test, while participating-media scattering, time-varying refraction and volumetric emission may require new physical/representation ownership contracts—**do not presume coverage or expand the current GM1 mechanism/batch**. Any proposed scope or architecture change returns to the human decision boundary.
- Make quality-matched external comparisons, including TransGI, only after reference, workload and accounting alignment. Plan durations from measured costs; venue planning does not relax gates. A path-traced re-render is **not** a Neural Full Recompute. Any learned dynamic-update superiority claim requires an actually invalidated external neural representation and its native T1 rebuild/retrain/refit lifecycle, quality-matched to ReST-GI; if native T0-state reuse is valid, measure that separately. Compare Cycles/Mitsuba spp-dependent full-scene re-rendering under an additional, explicitly labelled cost axis. See [binding baseline-role contract](BASELINE_ROLES_AND_EVIDENCE_STRATEGY.md#mandatory-baseline-terminology-and-comparison-gate).
